## Findings

Ranked by score = impact / effort (impact 1 to 5, effort S = 1, M = 2, L = 3), highest first.

### A-20261017-01 The routine runs without its credentials

- Evidence: the first pass of `node workspace/audits/tools/run-all.mjs` wrote `sources_configured: []` and every collector as unset ("PSI_API_KEY unset", "SITE_URL unset", and so on). The sidecar in this report comes from a pass with the laptop's `.env` names handed to the same command; it still has no `AUDIT_AGENT_KEY`, `CF_ZONE_ID` or `BING_WEBMASTER_API_KEY`, and `SITE_URL` is in no env file. `not_measured.schedule` reads "AUDIT_AGENT_KEY or SITE_URL unset", so whether the schedule switch says disabled could not be read.
- Impact: 4
- Effort: S
- Score: 4.0
- Fix: set `SITE_URL=https://matterofplace.com`, `AUDIT_AGENT_KEY` (minted in `/admin`), `CF_ZONE_ID` and `BING_WEBMASTER_API_KEY` in the routine's environment, and have the routine load the audit names itself (an `audit` profile in `app/scripts/load-env.mjs`, outside the audit's allowed paths). The agent key and the Bing key need the operator's hands.
- PR: proposal only (needs secrets and a file outside the allowed paths)

### A-20261017-02 SEO checkers crash inside the collector

- Evidence: the second pass wrote `seo.failed` as 13 lines of a crash, not checks: `Invalid environment: RATE_LIMIT_SALT Required` at `app/src/server/lib/env.ts:79`. With a salt set, the checker refused on four production-only secrets ("required when MOP_ENV is production"), because `env.ts` defaults to production. With `MOP_ENV=local` and a salt that hashes nothing, `check-seo.ts` printed 9 `ok` lines and exited 0; that pass is the sidecar here.
- Impact: 4
- Effort: S
- Score: 4.0
- Fix: `workspace/audits/tools/crawl.mjs` runs the checkers as local with a throwaway salt; `workspace/audits/tools/common.mjs` lets `run` take the child's environment. Proved on the branch: `node workspace/audits/tools/crawl.mjs` with no checker environment printed `seo: {"checks":9,"failed":[]}`.
- PR: https://github.com/AbdulrahmanAmer/matter-of-place/pull/451

### A-20261017-03 The Actions minutes gauge reads LIMIT on a public repository

- Evidence: `usage.actions_minutes` used 16518 of 2000, 825.9 percent, status LIMIT, over 3966 runs. `gh repo view --json visibility` answers `PUBLIC`; standard runners on a public repository are not billed, so the line raises an alarm that is not real and will train readers to ignore LIMIT.
- Impact: 3
- Effort: S
- Score: 3.0
- Fix: `workspace/audits/tools/collectors/github.mjs` reads the repository's visibility and, when public, the row becomes `info` with the minutes kept as detail. `limits.json` is mirrored by the daily health job's `gauges.ts` and pinned by `app/tests/unit/audit/usage.test.ts`, both outside the audit's allowed paths.
- PR: proposal only (touches files outside the allowed paths)

### A-20261017-04 Mobile LCP is over the 2.5 second target on three pages
