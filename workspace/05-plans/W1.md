# W1 — Workforce: AI employees that run Matter of Place from the operator's laptop

Lane: Operate the business · Stage: 3 BUILD · Owner: the orchestrator (operator, 2026-10-12: "no workflows, you get
everything done yourself"); the database security rules, the daemon's tool policy and approvals are critical (S62).
Depends on: B2 (database, `audit_log`), B7 (admin shell, admin API, agent keys, roles), B8 (jobs, `events`), B8b
(recipes), B9 (captions runner pattern), B11 (newsletter), B10 (social posting, waits on the operator's accounts).
Decisions applied: H34 and H86 (every operating agent runs on this laptop through the operator's Claude account, no
model key in the cloud), S63 (autonomous to live), S23 (a person approves social posts for 60 days), H87, H88.
Learned from the OmniSkipX team ("Jay", 2026-10-12): a queue table plus a chat table, a hidden-window daemon and a
separate watchdog task, an explicit tool policy per role, CLAUDE* variables stripped before a spawn, process-tree
kills, a dedicated browser profile for agents, approvals bound to a content hash, a fresh session after about six turns.

## Goal and observed exit
The operator hires an employee by telling the orchestrator or by writing in the team chat; each employee has a role,
instructions, the events that wake it and the actions it may take. A form, a request or an inquiry wakes the right
employees on the laptop; they read, draft and coordinate in one group chat and in direct threads, and anything that
leaves the company (a post, a send, a reply to a client) waits in Approvals for a person. The admin page
`/admin/office` and a desktop shortcut show every employee live (state, current task, last run); hovering one opens a
quick ask; the team chat holds everyone.
Observed exit: from the desktop shortcut the operator writes "@chief hire a newsletter editor" in the team chat; a
Newsletter editor appears in the office within a minute; a test inquiry on the public site wakes the Concierge, whose
draft reply appears in Approvals and whose note appears in the team chat.

## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs: rows of `events` (the catalog of architecture 3.6, read only); messages written by people in the admin; the
operator's signed-in Claude CLI on this laptop (no model key anywhere, H34).
Outputs: rows of `agents`, `agent_messages`, `agent_runs`, `agent_approvals`; admin pages Office and Approvals.
Invariants:
1. No model key exists in the cloud or in `.env`; every model call is `claude -p` on this laptop with the prompt on
   stdin (P-008, cortex pin), CLAUDE* variables removed from the child's environment, and the whole process tree killed
   at the time limit (taskkill /T /F).
2. Each role names its tools; a spawn passes `--strict-mcp-config` with an empty MCP config and `--allowedTools` from
   the role, so no worker inherits the operator's browser bridge or connectors (Jay's lesson 1).
3. Nothing leaves the company without a person: an employee proposes an outward action as an `agent_approvals` row
   bound to the SHA-256 of its exact content; only a staff member's approval executes it, and an edit after approval
   voids it.
4. A wake is data: `agents.wake_on` lists event types, edited in the admin; the daemon matches new `events` rows against
   it. No pipeline is hard-coded (CLAUDE.md, recipes stay as they are).
5. Every write goes through a security-definer SQL function that checks the caller's role; staff read through RLS;
   the public reads nothing.
6. One conversation per run, resumed for at most six turns, then a fresh session with a written summary.
7. The daemon and its watchdog run hidden (wscript and a hidden-window launcher), never flash a console.
Events emitted: none new; the daemon writes `audit_log` rows (`agent.hired`, `agent.run_started`, `agent.run_finished`,
`approval.decided`) as audit actions, not catalog events.
Permissions: `workforce.read` all staff; `workforce.chat` all staff; `workforce.hire` and `workforce.approve` admin and
chief_editor.

## Data changes (migrations, enums, RLS, seeds)
- `agents` (id, slug unique, name, role, instructions, model, wake_on text[], tools text[], state, current_task,
  last_seen_at, created_by, created_at). `agent_messages` (id, thread: `team` or an agent id, author_kind, author_id,
  body, created_at). `agent_runs` (id, agent_id, cause, event_id, message_id, state, lease_until, session_id, turns,
  summary, started_at, finished_at). `agent_approvals` (id, agent_id, run_id, action, content jsonb, content_hash,
  state, decided_by, decided_at).
- RLS on all four: staff select; no direct insert or update; writes through the functions of the Contract.
- Seed: Chief of staff (coordinates, may hire), Editor (requests and property copy), Concierge (inquiries), Social
  designer (post drafts), Newsletter editor (Place Notes drafts).

## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- P-008 (inline text loses backslashes), P-3431 (orphaned pipes), the cortex pin on `claude -p` and stdin.
- Jay's list: inherited MCP servers, inherited CLAUDE* variables, orphan children, EPERM renames, DNS blips,
  the python3 stub, PowerShell 5.1 quirks, two agents editing one repo, blanket approvals.
- Cost: every run spends the operator's Claude usage; the daemon caps runs per hour and the office shows the count.

## Out of scope
A visual node editor for recipes (its own plan), posting to social networks before the operator's accounts exist
(B10), employees editing the codebase.
