## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs
- Architecture 3 and its conventions: `uuid` ids from `gen_random_uuid()`, `timestamptz`, money as `numeric(12,2)` with a `char(3)` currency and a `check (currency = 'USD')`, `created_at`/`updated_at` with a trigger on every mutable table, RLS on every table.
- Enum names from the sketch (`docs/database/schema.sql`, reused by name only): `property_type`, `listing_status`, `campaign_tier`, `submission_source`, `editorial_state`, `media_orientation`, `story_category`, `inquiry_intent`, `inquiry_state`, `accepted_state`, `exposure_package`, `submission_state`, `channel_status`, `app_role`. Values differ where the architecture differs (below).
- Bundled content in `src/data/*` as the source of illustrative and reference rows (S30).

Outputs
- `supabase/migrations/*.sql` (12 files, listed under Data changes), `supabase/config.toml`, `supabase/seed.sql` (comment only).
- `src/db/types.ts` (generated, never hand edited), `src/db/index.ts` (helpers), `src/domain/rows.ts`, `src/domain/workflow.ts`, changes to `src/domain/contracts.ts`.
- `scripts/seed.ts`, `scripts/variants.ts`, `scripts/gen-types.mjs`, `scripts/lib/*`.
- Two Supabase projects, `mop-dev` and `mop-prod`, both linked and migrated.

Enum values fixed by architecture 3
- `app_role`: `chief_editor, managing_editor, visual_editor, media_ops, commercial, admin`. Replaces the sketch list and the `editorialRoles` display names in `contracts.ts` (G-004: `editorialRoles` becomes `appRoles` plus `roleLabels`).
- `submission_state` (column `workflow_state`): `Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Declined, Awaiting Assets, Invoice Issued, Scheduled, Published, Distribution Active, Completed`. `contracts.ts` `submissionStates` currently says `Awaiting Payment`; it changes to `Invoice Issued` in the same commit (G-004, S32).
- `editorial_state`: `draft, review, published, archived`. `actor_kind`: `human, agent`. `payment_status`: `due, paid, waived, refunded`. `payment_method`: `invoice_manual, stripe`. `channel_status`, `campaign_tier`, `listing_status` as the sketch.

Invariants
1. Nothing in the browser holds a Supabase key (architecture 1). No grant to `anon` on any table, function or sequence; `authenticated` gets only what an RLS policy allows. Public reads go through the Worker with the service role.
2. Every table has RLS enabled, and a table without a policy for a role is closed to that role. Default privileges for new tables revoke everything from `anon` and `authenticated`, so a forgotten grant fails closed.
3. `audit_log`, and later `events` and `automation_revisions`, are append-only: a trigger raises on update and delete even for the service role.
4. Only these delete paths exist: cascades from foreign keys, and `admin` deleting `agent_keys` (architecture 3.7).
5. A submission cannot reach `Invoice Issued` or later without `accepted_at`, and cannot reach `Scheduled` without a `payments` row whose status is `paid` or `waived` (S32). The transition graph is one function, mirrored by `src/domain/workflow.ts`, and a database test compares them.
6. `properties.editorial_state = 'published'` if and only if `published_at` is not null. `hero_rank` and `featured_rank` are unique among non-null values. `properties.market_slug` and `region_slug` must agree (composite foreign key).
7. Markets are exactly `california`, `new-york`, `florida` (brand guardrail: no other market). `subscribers.markets` is a subset of those three.
8. `settings.catalog_version` increases by one on every change to published catalog content, and never on its own update.
9. Free tier: 500 MB (P-009). `analytics_events` is partitioned monthly and pruned to 6 months (ASSUMED, about 24 MB per 120,000 events at 200 bytes a row, so at most 150 MB at that volume).

Events emitted: none (B8 owns `events`). Permissions: architecture 3.7, encoded in `20261001090900_rls.sql` and tested as a matrix. Summary: select for every staff role; submissions decide by chief_editor and managing_editor; properties write by chief_editor, managing_editor, visual_editor; payments by managing_editor and admin; `decline_reasons` by chief_editor, media_ops, admin; team tables by admin.

## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- G-010: `docs/database/schema.sql` is the Lovable sketch. Only enum names are reused; columns, types and policies come from architecture 3. Deleting the file at step 14 removes the temptation.
- G-004: the rule now spans `supabase/migrations`, `src/db/types.ts`, `src/domain/*` and the Zod contracts. Update the G-004 entry when this lands: its `paths:` should name `supabase/migrations/**` instead of `docs/database/schema.sql`, and its proof should be the `gen:types` diff plus `enums.check.ts`.
- G-011 and P-009: pg_cron, partitions and the `submissions` bucket are free. `mop-dev` and `mop-prod` each pause after 7 idle days; `mop-prod` needs the keep-warm before launch (B8b). Storage 1 GB: submission photographs at 25 MB each fill it after about 40 full-size uploads, so B7 must move accepted photographs to R2 and delete the originals from the bucket.
- G-008: the Supabase CLI on Windows can emit CRLF. `gen-types.mjs` normalises; the postwrite hook rejects CRLF otherwise.
- bun 1.3 skips dependency install scripts unless the package is in `trustedDependencies`. `supabase` downloads its binary in a postinstall; without the entry `bunx supabase` may work while `node_modules/.bin/supabase` does not. The global CLI 2.98.2 is a fallback.
- The `service_role` key bypasses RLS: the matrix proves user roles, not the Worker. The Worker's own checks are tested in B3 and B7.
- The `audit_log` immutability trigger also blocks the service role by design; a wrong row is corrected with a new row.
- Cross-plan: B8 lists `events` under its own migration, but B3 emits events before B8 lands. B3's `emitEvent` stub bridges the gap. B8b must not create `decline_reasons`.
- UNPROVEN until step 12: Bun loading `src/data/*` with the asset-loader plugin (imports of `.jpg`). Fallback: a one-off `scripts/lib/data-snapshot.ts` generated with Vite's SSR build; record the choice.
- ASSUMED region `us-east-1` for both projects; a project's region cannot change later.
