## Goal and observed exit
Place Notes is assembled automatically every 14 days from what was published since the last issue, reviewed and approved by a human on `/admin › Newsletter` (screen 13), and sent through Resend Broadcasts to confirmed subscribers only; Campaign-tier properties also get a standalone email to the property's market audience.
Observed exit (completion map B11, adjusted: it says weekly, S25 says every two weeks and wins): "broadcast delivered to a test audience." Proof: step 8 sends an approved issue to an audience of three test addresses and shows delivered events, then an unsubscribe on one address removes it from the next send.

## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs
- Event `asset.approved` for kind `newsletter_block` → step `queue_digest` (mode `add`). Event `digest.due` (emitted by the B8b scheduler when `schedule_settings.digest` is due, default every 14 days) → step `queue_digest` (mode `assemble`) then `notify_admin`. ASSUMED: one catalog step `queue_digest` with param `mode: "add" | "assemble"`, because the closed catalog of 14 steps has no separate "build issue draft" step even though architecture 5 says "build issue draft". B8b's seed recipe for `digest.due` must set `mode: "assemble"`.
- Event `subscriber.confirmed` (B5 owns the recipe that creates the Resend contact). B11 only relies on its outcome: `subscribers.confirmed_at` set, `resend_contact_id` stored, contact added to the audience `place-notes` and to `market-<ca|ny|fl>` for each market in `subscribers.markets`.
- Admin actions from screen 13: build, edit, preview, send test, approve (with optional `send_at`).
- Resend webhook rows from B5's `hooks/resend/events` (delivery, bounce, complaint, and, when tracking is on, open and click). ASSUMED B5 stores each event in `email_events` with the broadcast id; B11 aggregates it.
- Secrets: `RESEND_API_KEY` (tech-stack 4), in Supabase function secrets because the send job runs in the Edge Function.

Outputs
- `newsletter_issues` rows (architecture 3.6): `number`, `scheduled_for`, `status` `draft|approved|sending|sent`, `blocks jsonb`, `subject`, `resend_broadcast_id`, `metrics jsonb`, `approved_by`. ASSUMED extra columns `preheader text`, `sent_at`, `updated_at`, `send_error text`.
- `blocks` is an ordered array: `{ id, type: "intro" | "property" | "story", asset_id?, property_id?, story_id?, text? }`. Property blocks point at an approved `newsletter_block` asset; story blocks point at a published `stories` row.
- Resend objects: audiences (`place-notes`, `market-ca`, `market-ny`, `market-fl`), one Broadcast per issue and per standalone email, `resend_broadcast_id` stored. ASSUMED adapter risk: Resend has been reorganising Audiences into a newer contacts and segments model; the spec says Audiences and Broadcasts, the adapter `src/server/channels/resend.ts` is the only file that knows the API, and step 2 re-reads the current docs before coding.
- `newsletter_issues.metrics`: `{ recipients, delivered, bounced, complained, opened, clicked, unsubscribed, per_property: { <property_id>: { clicks } }, updated_at }`.
- Rendered issue HTML from `src/templates/email/place-notes.tsx` (masthead, optional intro line, property blocks via B9 `NewsletterBlock`, story blocks, footer with the Omnikom legal entity and postal address, Resend unsubscribe variable).

Invariants
1. Never send an empty issue. Assembly with zero eligible properties and zero eligible stories creates no draft, notifies admin "Nothing to send this cycle", and advances the schedule. Coming-soon reality (S30): the first issues will be editorial only or absent.
2. Recipients are exactly: `confirmed_at is not null and unsubscribed_at is null`. Anything else is never added to an audience. Unsubscribe reflected from Resend (webhook `contact.updated` or unsubscribe event) into `subscribers.unsubscribed_at` within the webhook's latency, and the contact is removed or flagged in the audience.
3. Human approval always. No agent can approve or send an issue (ASSUMED, conservative: S23 covers social posts only, email is irreversible and affects sender reputation). `approve` requires `actor_kind = human`. Screen 13 send-test is allowed for agents.
4. Footer completeness gate: the send job refuses when `siteConfig.legal.entity` or `siteConfig.legal.address` is null (B16 fills them) or when the unsubscribe variable is missing from the rendered HTML. Reason: postal address and working unsubscribe are legal requirements for commercial email (CAN-SPAM).
5. Free-tier gate (P-009): before sending, `assertQuota(recipients)` compares recipients plus today's transactional sends with the free 100-a-day limit and the month total with 3,000. If it would exceed either, the issue stays `approved`, admin is notified with the numbers, and nothing is split silently. A broadcast to more than about 100 contacts cannot leave in one day on the free tier; that is the first paid-tier trigger (Resend Pro, decision required per G-011). The check also blocks at 90 percent so bounces and transactional mail keep headroom.
6. Idempotent send: the send job key is `newsletter_send:<issue_id>:<n>`; the broadcast is created at most once (`resend_broadcast_id` written before send is triggered, and checked first). A crash after "create" resumes with "send".
7. Subject and preheader are built by rule, not by a model (S20): `Place Notes No. <n>: <first block title>` truncated at a word boundary to 60 characters, preheader = first block deck up to 110 characters, no em dashes, editable by a human before approval.
8. Link hygiene: every link in an issue carries `utm_source=place_notes&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-<n>&utm_content=<slug>` so B15 attribution and GA4 see it; no third-party redirect tracking beyond what Resend adds when open and click tracking are switched on (ASSUMED on for clicks, off for opens: opens are unreliable after mail privacy features and are recorded as soft).
9. Standalone email (Campaign tier): sent to `market-<property market>` only; requires the pending `standalone_email` asset to be approved on screen 10; approving that asset also approves the linked `waiting_approval` `send_email(standalone)` job in one click (ASSUMED link: matched by property id and step type), so a human does not approve twice.

Events emitted: none new. Consumed: `asset.approved`, `digest.due`, `subscriber.confirmed`.

Permissions (admin-screens 13, architecture 3.7)
| Action | Roles |
|---|---|
| list issues, subscribers tab, preview | all six roles (subscribers export: media_ops, managing_editor, chief_editor, ASSUMED because it is personal data) |
| build, edit blocks, subject, send test | media_ops, managing_editor, chief_editor |
| approve, schedule or send now | media_ops, managing_editor, chief_editor, humans only |
| agents | list, build, edit, preview, send test by bearer key, scope `newsletter`; never approve |

Spec gaps in architecture 4.2 that this slice adds: `PUT newsletter/issues/:id` (edit blocks, subject, preheader), `GET newsletter/subscribers` (counts by market, confirmed and pending, CSV export). Approve takes `send_at` (null means now), so no separate "send" route.

## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- P-009: 100 emails a day is the binding limit long before 3,000 a month. Transactional mail (received, accepted, invoice, confirmations) shares that day. At 70 contacts the plan needs a decision, not a surprise (G-011). Contacts cap 1,000.
- Resend product naming may have changed (Audiences and Broadcasts versus contacts and segments). Adapter isolation and step 2 are the guard. UNPROVEN until step 2.
- Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens; open rate is stored but not used for any decision, click counts are.
- Double opt-in is B5's. Until B5's confirm route works, the audience is empty and B11 cannot be proven; do not seed fake confirmed subscribers on production (S30).
- G-004: `src/domain/newsletter.ts` block types and `newsletter_issues.blocks` shape change together; `blocks` is jsonb so the Zod schema is the guard, add a DB check on `jsonb_typeof(blocks) = 'array'`.
- G-007: email HTML needs colours inline, so the template reads them from a small exported object generated from `tokens.css` at build (`src/templates/email/tokens.ts`, generated, never typed by hand) rather than copying hex.
- Timezone: `scheduled_for` and `send_at` are stored in UTC; the editor shows the owner's timezone from `settings`.
- Legal: the privacy page (B16) must say email addresses are held for Place Notes and that inquiries can be forwarded to Omnikom (B15). Otherwise footer and privacy disagree.
- The sender identity (`Place Notes <notes@matterofplace.com>`, ASSUMED) must be on the verified domain, and replies should land somewhere read (Cloudflare Email Routing, L1).
