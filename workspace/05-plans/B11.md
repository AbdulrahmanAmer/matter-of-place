## Goal and observed exit
Place Notes is assembled automatically every 14 days from what was published since the last issue, reviewed and approved by a human on `/admin › Newsletter` (screen 13), and sent through Resend Broadcasts to confirmed subscribers only; Campaign-tier properties also get a standalone email to the confirmed Place Notes subscribers of the property's market, and interest-only signups get one notice when their market opens (G15).
Observed exit (completion map B11): "broadcast delivered to a test audience." Proof: step 8 sends an approved issue to an audience of three test addresses and shows delivered events, then an unsubscribe on one address removes it from the next send.

Free-tier gate (P-009): before sending, `assertQuota(db, recipients, now)` adds the audience's `newsletter_recipient_count` to `email_sent_today()` (B5's function, which `<ts>_newsletter.sql` extends to count broadcast recipients too) and compares the sum with `settings.email.daily_cap` (B5, default 90, which is the 90 percent headroom of the free 100 a day); it adds the recipients to the month total (`email_messages` with `sent_at` this UTC month plus `broadcast_recipients_since(<start of month>)`) and compares with 2,700 (90 percent of 3,000). When today is the only problem the job returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: next UTC midnight plus 1 minute, reason: "quota" }` (no attempt consumed, B8 invariant 3) and enqueues one `notify_admin` with the numbers; when the recipients alone exceed the daily cap it throws `NonRetryableError("quota_exceeds_plan")`, writes `send_error` and enqueues one `notify_admin`. The issue stays `approved` and nothing is split silently. Every `notify_admin` this slice enqueues is `enqueueJob(db, { type: "notify_admin", idempotencyKey, params: { headline }, data: { summary, link_path } })` (G13; B5's `admin_notify` resolver reads them): quota, key `notify_admin:quota:<issue_id>:<UTC date>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> waits for tomorrow's quota", summary "<recipients> recipients, <sent today> sent today, daily cap <cap>", link `/admin/newsletter/<issue_id>`; plan, key `notify_admin:quota_plan:<issue_id>:<approval_count>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> exceeds the free sending plan", summary "<recipients> recipients, daily cap <cap>; sending needs a paid plan decision", same link. A standalone send uses the same two notices with `standalone:<asset_id>` in place of `<issue_id>`, headline "Standalone email for <property title> waits for tomorrow's quota" or "... exceeds the free sending plan", link `/admin/assets` (screen 10). A broadcast to more than about 90 contacts cannot leave in one day on the free tier; that is the first paid-tier trigger (Resend Pro, decision required per G-011).

12. Weekly KPI email to the CEO (GG-03). Every Saturday at 15:00 UTC B8b's `runDueSchedules` enqueues the system job `kpi_weekly` (handler `src/server/jobs/system/kpi-weekly.ts`) from the `schedule_settings` row `kpi_weekly` (seeded by B8b, `0 15 * * 6`, enabled; G9) with key `kpi_weekly:<UTC date>`, so screen 20 can pause it; this slice adds no pg_cron row. The job computes the KPIs for the last full ISO week (Monday to Sunday in `America/New_York`, ASSUMED owner zone) with the SQL function `kpi_weekly(p_week_start date)` and emails them to every address of `resolveAdminRecipients(db)` through B5's `sendOne` (one `email_messages` row per address, `template_key` `kpi_weekly`, so the daily cap counts it; a 429 returns `retry_at`). The definitions live in one file, `src/server/kpi/definitions.ts`, and are: submissions received (`submissions.received_at` in the week); acceptance rate (accepted decisions divided by accepted plus declined decisions made in the week, shown as "n of m", never a percentage of zero; a decision is an `events` row of type `submission.accepted` or `submission.declined`, dated by `events.at`, matched by `payload->>'submission_id'`); time to decision (median hours from `submissions.received_at` to the first such event of that submission); invoices issued and invoices paid (count and amount from `payments.issued_at` and `paid_at`, `void` excluded); properties published (`published_at`); posts by channel (`social_posts.posted_at`); newsletter growth (confirmed in the week, unsubscribed in the week, net, total confirmed); inquiries per published property (`inquiries.received_at` in the week, G23, divided by published properties, with the top five properties by count grouped on `inquiries.subject_slug` where `subject_kind = 'property'`, joined to `properties.slug` (B15's join; `inquiries` has no `property_id` column); "no published properties yet" when there are none). Each value shows the change against the previous week. No model writes it (S20). B14's audit report reads the same function so the two never disagree.
13. Resend contacts (ASSUMED A7, G14). `syncAudience(db, key)` in `src/server/newsletter/audience.ts` is the only code that adds or removes a contact; there is no trigger, step type or system job for contacts. Start: called by `newsletter-send.ts` (key `place-notes`) and `sendStandalone` (the market key) right before `createBroadcast`, and by the daily hygiene run for every key in `settings.resend.audiences` (invariant 10 c). Mechanism: `ensureAudience(db, key)`, then `listContacts(audienceId)` from Resend and `newsletter_audience_members(key)` from the database; `addContact` for each member whose email is not listed (a contact that already exists counts as added) and stores the returned id in `subscribers.resend_contact_id` when it is null (`newsletter_set_contact`); `removeContact` for each listed contact whose email is not a member (unsubscribed, archived, suppressed, interest-only, or anonymised by B7's `delete_subject`, whose original address no longer matches any row), then clears `resend_contact_id` on the row that held that contact id (`newsletter_clear_contact`). A second run with nothing changed makes no add or remove call. Returns `{ added, removed, members }`. Forgetting (CCPA, B3 GP-01): an anonymised subscriber's contact is removed by the next hygiene run (daily) or broadcast, whichever comes first; no address is ever written to a job payload. Failures: `RateLimited` (429) makes the caller return `retry_at` from `Retry-After`, `Transient` (5xx, network) throws (B8 backoff), other 4xx throw `NonRetryableError`; with `EMAIL_DRY_RUN=1` it makes no call and writes nothing (returns `{ dry_run: true, members }`), and without `RESEND_API_KEY` it throws `NonRetryableError("resend_not_configured")` (B5's convention). ## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- P-009: 100 emails a day is the binding limit long before 3,000 a month. Transactional mail (received, accepted, invoice, confirmations) shares that day. At 70 contacts the plan needs a decision, not a surprise (G-011). Contacts cap 1,000.
- Resend product naming may have changed (Audiences and Broadcasts versus contacts and segments). Adapter isolation and step 2 are the guard. UNPROVEN until step 2.
- Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens; open rate is stored but not used for any decision, click counts are.
- Double opt-in is B5's. Until B5's confirm route works and the Resend account exists (none yet), the audience is empty and the real-send proofs cannot run; do not seed fake confirmed subscribers on production (S30).
- G-004: `src/domain/newsletter.ts` block types and `newsletter_issues.blocks` shape change together; `blocks` is jsonb so the Zod schema is the guard, backed by the DB check `jsonb_typeof(blocks) = 'array'`.
- G-007: email HTML needs colours inline, so the issue template reads them from B5's generated `src/templates/theme.gen.ts` (`scripts/gen-theme.ts`, from `tokens.css`) rather than copying hex; this slice creates no tokens file.
- Timezone: `scheduled_for` (which holds `send_at`) is stored in UTC; the editor shows the owner's timezone from `settings`.
- Contacts are reconciled, not pushed (G14): a confirmed subscriber becomes a Resend contact only at the next `syncAudience` run, and an anonymised one leaves Resend at the next daily hygiene run or broadcast. If an admin pauses `newsletter_hygiene` on screen 20, CCPA erasure in Resend waits for the next broadcast; the 45-day clock (G29) leaves room, and the runbook says to keep the row enabled.
- Two Resend environments, one account: the `dev-` audience prefix keeps `mop-dev` test sends away from production subscribers; a builder who removes it can mail real people from a test.
- Legal: the privacy page (B16) must say email addresses are held for Place Notes and that inquiries can be forwarded to Omnikom (B15). Otherwise footer and privacy disagree.
- GG-05: re-permission mail counts toward the 100 a day of P-009: it is sent by B5's `send_email`, whose daily cap reads `email_sent_today()`, which counts broadcasts too after this slice; the 20 a day cap keeps it from starving transactional mail. Lapsing a silent subscriber is irreversible for us (they can sign up again); the thirty day wait and B5's audit row are the safeguards. Engagement uses clicks only, so a subscriber who reads without clicking is asked once a year, which the copy must make painless.
- GG-06: the preview reaches the same inbox that receives `notify_admin`; if the recipients list (B7 screen 24) is empty the job fails loudly rather than skipping, because a silent missing preview defeats its purpose. The preview is of the frozen issue; approval locks edits, so what arrives is what sends.
- GG-03: the KPIs are counts and medians over tables that are empty before launch (S30), so the first emails say zero; that is correct. "Week" is ISO in the owner zone; if the CEO wants another day or zone, it is one constant in `definitions.ts`. Payment amounts in the email are the CEO's own figures, sent only to the recipients in `settings`; B5 must not log the body. B14 uses the same SQL function for its report.
- The sender identity (`settings.resend.from_notes`, ASSUMED `Place Notes <notes@matterofplace.com>` when set, else B5's `RESEND_FROM`) must be on the verified domain, and replies should land somewhere read (the Zoho mailbox admin@matterofplace.com; Cloudflare Email Routing is not used, L1 step 4f).
