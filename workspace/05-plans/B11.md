## Goal and observed exit
Place Notes is assembled automatically every 14 days from what was published since the last issue, reviewed and approved by a human on `/admin › Newsletter` (screen 13), and sent through Resend Broadcasts to confirmed subscribers only; Campaign-tier properties also get a standalone email to the confirmed Place Notes subscribers of the property's market, and interest-only signups get one notice when their market opens (G15).
Observed exit (completion map B11): "broadcast delivered to a test audience." Proof: step 8 sends an approved issue to an audience of three test addresses and shows delivered events, then an unsubscribe on one address removes it from the next send.

Invariants
1. Never send an empty issue. `queue_digest` in mode `assemble` with zero eligible properties, zero eligible stories and no open draft holding blocks creates no draft and returns `{ status: "done", result: { issue_id: null } }`; the recipe's `notify_admin` then says "Nothing to send this cycle" (the `digest.due` change to B5's resolver, Files). The schedule is advanced by B8b's `runDueSchedules` claim (`last_run_at`, `next_run_at`), not by this slice. Coming-soon reality (S30): the first issues will be editorial only or absent.
2. Recipients of an audience are exactly: `confirmed_at is not null and unsubscribed_at is null and archived_at is null`, the address does not end with `.invalid` (B7's `delete_subject` anonymisation) and is not in `email_suppressions` (B5: hard bounces and spam complaints, GG-05), and `source not like 'interest:%'` (B3b invariant 7, G15); for `market-<x>` also the market slug of `MARKET_AUDIENCE` in `markets` (`<market slug> = any(markets)`). SQL `newsletter_audience_members(p_audience text)` returns them (`id`, `email`, `resend_contact_id`) and `newsletter_recipient_count(p_audience text)` counts them. Anything else is never added to an audience. There is no Matter of Place unsubscribe endpoint.

5. Free-tier gate (P-009): a broadcast is `bulk` mail (Inputs, mail classes), so before sending, `assertQuota(db, recipients, now)` adds the audience's `newsletter_recipient_count` to `email_sent_today()` (B5's function, which `<ts>_newsletter.sql` extends to count broadcast recipients too) and compares the sum with `settings.email.bulk_cap` (B5; 50 in production, INT-03), never with `daily_cap`, so a broadcast always leaves the rest of the day to invoices, acknowledgements, confirmations and alerts; it adds the recipients to `email_sent_month()` (B5's function, which `<ts>_newsletter.sql` extends the same way with `broadcast_recipients_since(<start of the UTC month>)`) and compares with `settings.email.monthly_cap` (B5; 2,600 in production). When today is the only problem the job returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: next UTC midnight plus 1 minute, reason: "quota" }` (no attempt consumed, B8 invariant 3) and enqueues one `notify_admin` with the numbers; when the recipients alone exceed `bulk_cap` it throws `NonRetryableError("quota_exceeds_plan")`, writes `send_error` and enqueues one `notify_admin`. The issue stays `approved` and nothing is split silently. Every `notify_admin` this slice enqueues is `enqueueJob(db, { type: "notify_admin", idempotencyKey, params: { headline }, data: { summary, link_path } })` (G13; B5's `admin_notify` resolver reads them): quota, key `notify_admin:quota:<issue_id>:<UTC date>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> waits for tomorrow's quota", summary "<recipients> recipients, <sent today> sent today, bulk cap <cap>", link `/admin/newsletter/<issue_id>`; plan, key `notify_admin:quota_plan:<issue_id>:<approval_count>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> exceeds the free sending plan", summary "<recipients> recipients, bulk cap <cap>; sending needs a paid plan decision", same link. A standalone send uses the same two notices with `standalone:<asset_id>` in place of `<issue_id>`, headline "Standalone email for <property title> waits for tomorrow's quota" or "... exceeds the free sending plan", link `/admin/assets` (screen 10). A broadcast to more contacts than `bulk_cap` (about 50) cannot leave in one day on the free tier; that is the first paid-tier trigger (Resend Pro, decision required per G-011).
6. Idempotent send: `newsletter_approve_issue` enqueues the system job `newsletter_send` with key `newsletter_send:<issue_id>:<approval_count>` and `run_after = scheduled_for`; the broadcast is created at most once (`resend_broadcast_id` written right after create and before send, and checked first), and sent at most once (INT-10): when `resend_broadcast_id` is already set, the handler calls `getBroadcast(id)` before the quota gate, `syncAudience` and send; a status other than `draft` (`queued`, `scheduled`, `sending`, `sent`) means Resend already accepted the send, so it calls `newsletter_mark_sent(issue, newsletter_recipient_count('place-notes'))` and returns `{ adopted: true }` with no send call; only a `draft` broadcast is sent. A `Conflict` answer from `sendBroadcast` is confirmed through `getBroadcast` and treated the same way. A crash after "create" therefore resumes with "send", and a crash after "send" ends `sent` without a second send. The handler exits `{ skipped: "stale" }` when the issue is no longer `approved` or `sending` or its `approval_count` differs from the job's; `{ skipped: "channel_disabled" }` when `channel_settings.newsletter.enabled` is false, with one `notify_admin` (key `notify_admin:newsletter_disabled:<issue_id>:<approval_count>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> not sent: the newsletter channel is off", summary "Switch the newsletter channel on, then approve the issue again", link `/admin/automation/settings`). Before create it re-reads every block: when a property block's property is no longer `editorial_state = 'published'` with `taken_down_at` null, or a story block's story is no longer `published`, it writes `send_error` naming those block ids, enqueues one `notify_admin` (key `notify_admin:block_unpublished:<issue_id>:<approval_count>`, headline "Place Notes No. <n> holds an unpublished item", summary the block titles, link `/admin/newsletter/<issue_id>`) and throws `NonRetryableError("block_unpublished")`; the issue stays `approved`, so a human unapproves, removes the block and approves again (what arrives is what was approved). Dry run (`EMAIL_DRY_RUN=1`, G14): every gate runs, `syncAudience` makes no call, the adapter returns `dry_<uuid>` for create and send, and the issue ends `sent` with `resend_broadcast_id = 'dry_<uuid>'`, `sent_at` and `metrics.recipients` set; `broadcast_recipients_since` skips every issue and asset whose broadcast id starts with `dry_`, so a dry run never counts toward the cap (as B5 leaves `sent_at` null on its dry rows). Status moves `approved` → `sending` before create (`newsletter_mark_sending`) and `sending` → `sent` with `sent_at = now()` when `sendBroadcast` returns 2xx (`newsletter_mark_sent`); the broadcast id is written by `newsletter_set_broadcast_id` and `send_error` by `newsletter_set_send_error` (Data changes); there is no poll and no webhook wait. Resend errors are classified on the body's `name` first and the status second, through B5's table `RESEND_ERRORS` and its `classifyResendError(status, body, headers, now)` in `src/server/email/resend-errors.ts`, which `resend.ts` imports (INT-11): `rate_limit_exceeded` and 503 give `retry_at` at now plus the larger of `Retry-After` and 2 seconds; `daily_quota_exceeded` gives `retry_at` at the next UTC midnight plus 1 minute; `monthly_quota_exceeded` gives `retry_at` on the 1st of next month; `concurrent_idempotent_requests` and `resource_locked` give `retry_at` in 30 seconds; `missing_api_key`, `restricted_api_key`, `suspended_api_key`, `invalid_permission`, a 403 `validation_error` and `invalid_idempotency_key` throw `NonRetryableError(<name>)` after writing `send_error`; an unknown name falls back to the status: 429 `retry_at`, 5xx and network errors throw (B8 backoff), other 4xx throw `NonRetryableError` after writing `send_error`.

The definitions live in one file, `src/server/kpi/definitions.ts`, and are: submissions received (`submissions.received_at` in the week); acceptance rate (accepted decisions divided by accepted plus declined decisions made in the week, shown as "n of m", never a percentage of zero; a decision is an `events` row of type `submission.accepted` or `submission.declined`, dated by `events.at`, matched by `payload->>'submission_id'`); time to decision (median hours from `submissions.received_at` to the first such event of that submission); invoices issued and invoices paid (count and amount from `payments.issued_at` and `paid_at`, `void` excluded); properties published (`published_at`); posts by channel (`social_posts.posted_at`); newsletter growth (confirmed in the week, unsubscribed in the week, net, total confirmed); inquiries per published property (`inquiries.received_at` in the week, G23, divided by published properties, with the top five properties by count grouped on `inquiries.subject_slug` where `subject_kind = 'property'`, joined to `properties.slug` (B15's join; `inquiries` has no `property_id` column); "no published properties yet" when there are none). Each value shows the change against the previous week. No model writes it (S20). B14's audit report reads the same function so the two never disagree.

- P-009: 100 emails a day is the binding limit long before 3,000 a month, and it is per Resend account, shared by `mop-dev`, production and Supabase Auth's magic links (INT-02). Bulk mail (broadcasts, standalone, market-open notices, previews, re-permission) stops at `settings.email.bulk_cap`, so transactional mail (received, accepted, invoice, confirmations) and alerts always keep the rest of the day (Inputs, mail classes). At about 35 contacts (70 percent of `bulk_cap`) the plan needs a decision, not a surprise (G-011). Contacts cap 1,000.
- Deno imports (CS-01): the job runner loads this slice's system jobs and, through them, `src/server/newsletter/**`, `src/server/kpi/**`, `src/server/channels/resend.ts` and the email templates; every relative, `@/server/` or `@/domain/` import in those files carries the `.ts` extension (or `.tsx` for a template), or the `deno` step of the CI `check` job goes red.
- Resend product naming may have changed (Audiences and Broadcasts versus contacts and segments). Adapter isolation and step 2 are the guard. UNPROVEN until step 2.
- Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens; open rate is stored but not used for any decision, click counts are.
- Double opt-in is B5's. Until B5's confirm route works on `mop-dev` with `EMAIL_LIVE=1` (the Resend account exists, E17), the audience is empty and the real-send proofs cannot run; do not seed fake confirmed subscribers on production (S30).
- G-004: `src/domain/newsletter.ts` block types and `newsletter_issues.blocks` shape change together; `blocks` is jsonb so the Zod schema is the guard, backed by the DB check `jsonb_typeof(blocks) = 'array'`.
- G-007: email HTML needs colours inline, so the issue template reads them from B5's generated `src/templates/theme.gen.ts` (`scripts/gen-theme.ts`, from `tokens.css`) rather than copying hex; this slice creates no tokens file.
- Timezone: `scheduled_for` (which holds `send_at`) is stored in UTC; the editor shows the owner's timezone from `settings`.
- Contacts are reconciled, not pushed (G14): a confirmed subscriber becomes a Resend contact only at the next `syncAudience` run, and an anonymised one leaves Resend at the next daily hygiene run or broadcast. If an admin pauses `newsletter_hygiene` on screen 20, CCPA erasure in Resend waits for the next broadcast; the 45-day clock (G29) leaves room, and the runbook says to keep the row enabled.
- Two Resend environments, one account: the `dev-` audience prefix keeps `mop-dev` test sends away from production subscribers; a builder who removes it can mail real people from a test.
- Legal: the privacy page (B16) must say email addresses are held for Place Notes and that inquiries can be forwarded to Omnikom (B15, which is built and stays switched off because no Omnikom endpoint exists yet, S59). Otherwise footer and privacy disagree.
- GG-05: re-permission mail counts toward the 100 a day of P-009: it is sent by B5's `send_email`, whose daily cap reads `email_sent_today()`, which counts broadcasts too after this slice; the 20 a day cap keeps it from starving transactional mail. Lapsing a silent subscriber is irreversible for us (they can sign up again); the thirty day wait and B5's audit row are the safeguards. Engagement uses clicks only, so a subscriber who reads without clicking is asked once a year, which the copy must make painless.
- GG-06: the preview reaches the same inbox that receives `notify_admin`; if the recipients list (B7 screen 24) is empty the job fails loudly rather than skipping, because a silent missing preview defeats its purpose. The preview is of the frozen issue; approval locks edits, so what arrives is what sends.
- GG-03: the KPIs are counts and medians over tables that are empty before launch (S30), so the first emails say zero; that is correct. "Week" is ISO in the owner zone; if the CEO wants another day or zone, it is one constant in `definitions.ts`. Payment amounts in the email are the CEO's own figures, sent only to the recipients in `settings`; B5 must not log the body. B14 uses the same SQL function for its report.
- The sender identity is `RESEND_FROM_BULK`, `Place Notes <hello@notes.matterofplace.com>`, on the verified `notes.` domain (ruling H29, E18): nothing receives mail there, so replies go to `reply_to` `hello@matterofplace.com`, a Zoho alias of the admin mailbox (E19; Cloudflare Email Routing is not used, L1 step 4f), and a complaint against Place Notes touches neither `notify.` nor the root.
