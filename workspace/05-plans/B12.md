## Goal and observed exit
For a Campaign-tier property the publish recipe produces one vertical reel of about 18 seconds: real photographs in camera moves, typography as the actor, sound design only, a poster still, both stored in R2 and shown as a pending asset with a video player on screen 10; once approved it attaches to the dossier page and is available to the Instagram step (`post_meta`); X and LinkedIn do not get the reel at launch (S48).
Observed exit (completion map B12, tightened): "MP4 renders with the wordmark end card and on-screen text (no speech, so no captions track, G30)." Proof: the reel of the fixture property, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC, passes the motion gate with reel thresholds, passes the audio checks (loudness, no pitched content), and a contact sheet shows the wordmark end card and on-screen text. Silence from music is proven by inspection of the sound engine, not by the ear: `launch/engine/runtime/audio.js` uses noise, filters and envelopes only.

## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs
- Event `property.published` with `payload.tier = "Campaign"` → step `render_reel` (recipe condition `tiers: ["Campaign"]`, architecture 5). Heavy step: dispatched to `render.yml`.
- Job data (B9 Contract, B7 emits it, B8 passes it as `payload.data`): `{ property_id, slug, tier, market, submission_id?, revision? }`.
- Render spec, built only by `buildReelSpec(property, media)` in `src/server/assets/reel-spec.ts`, which lives under `src/server` so the job-runner's import map (`@mop/server/*`, B8) resolves it: `{ kind: "reel", property: { title, city, state, market, price, currency, beds, baths, interiorSqFt, type }, shots: [{ key, alt, orientation }] (4 photographs plus 6 tiles for the grid), copy: { location, title, facts, price, site, credit }, sound: { bed: "wind" | "water" | "room" | "city", seed: number } }`. `buildReelSpec` is pure: `render-reel.ts` selects, through `ctx.db`, the `properties` row (`title`, `city`, `state`, `market_slug`, `price`, `currency`, `beds`, `baths`, `interior_sq_ft`, `type`, `campaign_tier`, `editorial_state`) and the `property_media` rows (`r2_key`, `alt`, `orientation`, `sort_order`, ordered by `sort_order`) for `data.property_id`, and passes the rows to it. `spec_hash = specHash(spec)` imported from B9's `src/server/assets/spec.ts`, which this slice does not change. `shots[].key` is the row's `r2_key`, the EXIF-stripped original that B9's `render_variants` stored in R2 (G42, G25), for landscape and portrait photographs alike, asserted by `reel-spec.test.ts`; no variant is read and there is no extra size for the reel (G42); the scene scales each original to cover the 1080x1920 frame (Storyboard notes). A row whose `r2_key` is null is still staged and has no `variants` either (B9's `onResult` writes both in one update), so the variants wait of invariant 9 holds the step until every row has its stripped original; the spec never carries a URL, the runner resolves keys to local files (see `scripts/render-reel.mjs`). Shot choice: media in `sort_order`; the first 4 are the camera shots and the next 6 are the grid tiles; with 4 to 9 photographs the tiles reuse the shots in order; fewer than 4 is refused by the step (invariant 9).
- `sound.bed` comes from `bedFor(type, market)` in `reel-spec.ts`, one exhaustive table (ASSUMED, tunable there): `Waterfront` in any market gets water; `Apartment` or `Penthouse` in `new-york` gets city; `Apartment` or `Penthouse` in `california` or `florida` gets room; `Estate`, `Residence`, `Townhouse` or `Farmhouse` in `new-york` gets room; the same four in `california` or `florida` get wind. `seed` = first 8 hex digits of the property id, so a re-render is byte-for-byte reproducible.
- `copy` comes from `reelCopy(property)` in `reel-spec.ts` and is every string the scene draws (invariant 5): `location` (city and state, uppercase), `title`, `facts` ("5 BED · 4.5 BATH · 4,320 SF"), `price`, `site` (`matterofplace.com`), `credit` ("A product of Omnikom", the start of B16's `t.footer.line` in `src/lib/strings.ts`, asserted by `copy.test.ts`).
- Photographs are the EXIF-stripped originals that B9's `render_variants` stored in R2 (`property_media.r2_key`, G42), never an unstripped upload from the private `submissions` bucket; variants are not used for the reel. R2 is off until the operator enables it (S50, E8), so until then only the fixture spec with local image paths runs; every step that reads or writes R2 is marked BLOCKED below.

Outputs

- On approval: the trigger `assets_reel_video` (Data changes) calls `attach_reel`, which writes B2's column `properties.video jsonb` (G23) as `{ src, poster, caption, duration }`: `src` and `poster` are the R2 keys of the video and the poster (never URLs, ASSUMED A13), `caption` text, `duration` text in display form "0:18". This slice adds no column and changes no domain field or contract, so G-004 is not engaged.
- Event: `asset.approved` (B9). Instagram publishing of the reel is B10's mapping row.
- Failure path: a gate failure or a size mismatch is reported as `{ status: "failed", error: "gate_failed: <check> <value>", retryable: false }`, where `<check>` is one of the fixed names `format`, `fps`, `duration`, `coverage`, `static`, `cuts`, `avg_shot`, `audio`, `loudness`, `true_peak`, `flatness` (the first failed check of the gate's JSON) or `probe` (the `ffprobe` check of `render-reel.mjs`), and `<value>` is that check's measured value, so the job goes straight to `dead` (B8 Contract) and B9's trigger `assets_job_error` copies the error into `assets.render_error`; the asset stays `pending` and screen 10 shows Re-render. A runner lost without any callback is failed by B8's reaper after 30 minutes and retried under the same policy.

Invariants
1. Campaign only (S24): `render_reel` re-reads `properties.campaign_tier` (it does not trust `data.tier` alone) and throws `NonRetryableError("not_campaign")` for anything else, whatever the recipe says; an unpublished property throws `NonRetryableError("not_published")`, as B9's render steps do.
2. No music, no exceptions (S36). Sound is produced by `launch/engine/runtime/audio.js` from a cue list: noise beds and one-shots (air, room, wind, water, city, step, door, paper, fabric, whoosh, impact). No oscillator node exists in that module; a test greps for `createOscillator`, `OscillatorNode`, `Tone.` and fails on any hit. Rules from MOTION-BIBLE §3 hold: no pitched sustained tone, chord, melody, rhythmic loop or vocal; silence is a beat.
3. Moves come only from MOTION-BIBLE §2 (M1 to M13). Forbidden list applies: no bounce, elastic, spin, particles, lens flare, gradients, glow, counters, zoom-blur, stock wipes. Easing `power2.out` arrivals, `power3.inOut` camera, `expo.out` masks, `none` for camera moves on photographs (P-025), springs only on M10 (`back.out` with a strength of 1.3 or less, inside the scene's `gridAssemble` function and nowhere else). The scene may also call the helpers it imports from `launch/engine/runtime/film.js` (`maskIn`, `maskOut`, `track`, `weight`, `wipe`, `move`), whose default eases are the launch film's approved set: `expo.out`, `power3.in`, `power3.out`, `power1.out`, `power1.inOut`, `power3.inOut` and the timeline default `power2.out`. M11 (light sweep) is drawn by the function `lightSweep` as a solid Bone layer with `filter: blur(60px)` at a fixed low opacity, moved diagonally once at `ease: "none"`; the legibility layer under type set over a photograph is drawn by the function `scrim` as a solid Obsidian layer at a fixed opacity (functional, S51). Neither uses a CSS gradient. `tests/unit/reel/moves.test.ts` reads `launch/reel/scene.mjs` and `launch/reel/scene.html` and fails on any `ease:` value outside the scene list above and on the text `bounce`, `elastic`, `rough`, `particle`, `gradient` or `glow` in either file; it also reads `launch/engine/runtime/film.js` and fails when any `ease` value there (helper defaults included) is outside the helper set above, so a changed helper default goes red too.
4. Quiet means palette, type and copy, never a static screen (CLAUDE.md, S37): the reel gate requires motion in at least 85 percent of frames and no static run above 1.0 second.
5. Copy on screen is calm and factual: wordmark, LOCATION (uppercase Jost, tracking), title (Cormorant), facts line ("5 BED · 4.5 BATH · 4,320 SF"), price, `matterofplace.com`, "A product of Omnikom" (S33 wording as B16 fixes it). No "JUST LISTED", no exclamation, no em dash, no guarantee. The scene draws only the strings in `spec.copy` (built by `reelCopy`); `tests/unit/reel/copy.test.ts` checks `reelCopy` output and the `copy` block of `launch/reel/fixture-spec.json`: no em dash (U+2014), no `!`, no `JUST LISTED`, and every string passes B9's `lintCaption(text, property, "instagram")`.
6. Deterministic render: no wall clock (the runtime seeks a paused GSAP master, `engine/runtime/film.js`), seeded noise, fixed frame rate. Same spec hash, same MP4 bytes up to encoder version. Proved twice: `reel-spec.test.ts` (same input gives the same `specHash`, a changed bed gives a different one) and step 4 (stills captured twice from the same spec have the same SHA-256).
7. Free tier (P-009): about 3 minutes of Actions per reel is a measured planning figure from the launch engine; the guideline is at most 10 reels a month at launch (about 30 to 50 minutes of 2,000). It is a guideline the audit robot reports, never a cap in code (G30): Campaign is the only tier that renders a reel (invariant 1), and B14's weekly audit report carries `reels this month` (the count of `jobs` with `type = 'render_reel'` and `status = 'done'` since the first of the month) against the guideline of 10. The per-run minutes are the figure recorded in step 6, because the Actions minutes line may read "Not measured" (G11). Frames are deleted on the runner as soon as the MP4 verifies (P-016).
8. The step never posts. Output is `pending` until approved (S23): `onResult` writes only `files`, `meta` and `render_error` (cleared), through B9's `set_asset_files` and `set_asset_text` (null caption and alt text), never `status`, `caption` or `alt_text`, and enqueues no job; `render-reel.test.ts` asserts the asset is still `pending` and no `post_meta`, `post_x` or `post_linkedin` job exists after `onResult`.
9. Variants first (B8b invariant 6, the same rule as B9 invariant 10): `render_reel.run` calls `upsert_asset_stub` first; a recipe job (no `data.revision`) whose stub comes back `approved` or `published` returns `{ status: "done", result: { skipped: "already_approved" } }` without a dispatch and writes nothing to the asset (B9 invariant 12: a second publish never overwrites approved work; a new reel comes only through Re-render). Otherwise it throws `NonRetryableError("too_few_photos")` when the property has fewer than 4 `property_media` rows, then calls `variantsReady(db, property_id, "hero")` and `variantsReady(db, property_id, "carousel")` from B9's `spec.ts`; while either is false it returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: now + 2 minutes, reason: "variants_pending" }` (no attempt consumed). The wait is measured from the `created_at` of the reel stub, and after 60 minutes the step throws `NonRetryableError("variants_missing")`. Only then does it build the spec and call `dispatchHeavy(ctx, job, { spec, spec_hash, revision, asset_id })` (B8; `asset_id` is the id of the stub row): B8 merges the third argument into `client_payload.payload.data`, so the runner finds the spec at `payload.data.spec` of `MOP_JOB`, and on a 204 B8 stores the same object in `jobs.result`, so `onResult` reads `job.result.asset_id` and `job.result.spec_hash` and writes through B9's `set_asset_files` and `set_asset_text` only (Files, `render-reel.ts`; G43).

Permissions: as B9 for the asset actions (media_ops, chief_editor). The reel player on screen 10 is read-only for other roles.

## Storyboard of the reel (the storyboard file states this table; timings are the design, the review loop may move them)
Format 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 18.0 s = 540 frames. Palette, type, copy fixed by CLAUDE.md; composition, rhythm and photo choice are the producer's call (MOTION-BIBLE §8).
| Beat | s | Picture | Moves | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0 to 3.0 | photograph 1 fills the frame from frame 0, in motion | M6 push-in 6 to 9 percent, M8 rack focus 8 px to 0 in the first second, M2 LOCATION label opens | bed (wind, water, room or city per rule) fades in |
| 2 | 3.0 to 3.7 | frame divides and reveals photograph 2 | M9 split slide 0.7 s | whoosh |
| 3 | 3.7 to 7.0 | photograph 2, title over it | M7 crane (vertical drift, rotation 1.5 degrees or less), M1 mask reveal of the title, M3 weight shift | bed continues, a stone step for interiors |
| 4 | 7.0 to 7.7 | split into photograph 3 | M9 | whoosh |
| 5 | 7.7 to 11.0 | photograph 3, facts row | M6, M4 line wipe draws under the facts, M1 masks the row in | paper crackle, quiet |
| 6 | 11.0 to 11.2 | Obsidian, 6 frames | M13 hard cut to black | whoosh into a silence beat of 0.5 s |
| 7 | 11.2 to 15.4 | six tiles assemble, one grows to full frame | M10 (1.4 s) then M6 on the full tile | fabric, whoosh, bed returns |
| 8 | 15.4 to 18.0 | last photograph moving underneath, price and `matterofplace.com` mask in, the wordmark locks | M1, M2, M5 fast, M11 light sweep once | impact (non-pitched, under 80 Hz) on the lock, room tone out |
Notes: one hold on a flat field never exceeds about 0.8 s and an end card always has a moving photograph under it (GOTCHAS P-025). Camera moves on photographs run at constant speed (`ease: "none"`) so the gate does not read eased-out moves as static. A landscape photograph in a 9:16 frame is scaled to about 1.2 times cover and centred; horizontal pan is not in the vocabulary and is not used.

## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- P-024 (sound: the pitch check on noise beds) and P-025 (motion: type holds): measure flatness on the normalised AAC mix; give narrow beds a broadband layer; camera moves at constant speed; never a long hold on a flat field.
- P-016 (frames eat disk): a 1080×1920 PNG frame is heavier than the 1080p one; 540 frames can reach a gigabyte and a half on the runner; delete after verify; never leave a rejected render on a developer disk.
- P-017 (process entry, headless workers): the render job must never end its turn while ffmpeg or capture runs; `render-reel.mjs` runs everything in the foreground and only then writes `result.json`, which the workflow's `post-callback.mjs` step posts.
- P-026: this ffmpeg has no glob input, so the sheet and stills use `sheet.mjs`; the encode uses `%05d.png`, not a glob. Node ESM absolute Windows paths need `file://`; the scripts import by bare name from inside `launch/`.
- G-012: `render.yml` at the repository root; `launch/` is at the root beside the app, so the reel job needs `bun install` in `launch/` and does not use the app's `node_modules`.
- G-011 and P-009: 2,000 Actions minutes. A reel at 5 minutes and 10 a month is 50 minutes; measured in step 6 and recorded. The capture already runs at `--workers 2` on the two-core runner. If a run exceeds the step's 18 minutes, raise `timeout-minutes` and accept a longer run before cutting quality; 24 fps is NOT allowed (gate and Instagram both prefer 30).
- Photo source (G42): shots read the stripped originals in `property_media.r2_key`, downloaded by `render-reel.mjs` into `.tmp/reel/shots/` and scaled to cover the 1080x1920 frame; there is no extra variant for the reel. An original is as large as the upload was, so its download time counts toward the step's 18 minutes measured in step 6.
- Photo depth: MOTION-BIBLE M6 prefers three parallax planes; automatic plane separation would need a model (S20 forbids), so the reel uses single-plane pushes and cuts, which the vocabulary allows. A later slice can add hand-cut planes for hero shots.
- Reel template quality is the risk that S37 names ("v0 looked like a slideshow"). Do not skip step 5.
- Chrome on the runner: `google-chrome` is preinstalled on `ubuntu-latest` (ASSUMED, confirm); fonts come from the repo files, never from Google Fonts.
- Sound rules apply to captions too: captions never suggest audio ("turn sound on").
