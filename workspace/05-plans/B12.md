## Goal and observed exit
For a Campaign-tier property the publish recipe produces one vertical reel of about 18 seconds: real photographs in camera moves, typography as the actor, sound design only, a poster still, both stored in R2 and shown as a pending asset with a video player on screen 10; once approved it attaches to the dossier page and is available to the Instagram step (`post_meta`); X and LinkedIn do not get the reel at launch (S48).
Observed exit (completion map B12, tightened): "MP4 renders with wordmark and captions." Proof: the reel of the fixture property, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC, passes the motion gate with reel thresholds, passes the audio checks (loudness, no pitched content), and a contact sheet shows the wordmark end card and on-screen text. Silence from music is proven by inspection of the sound engine, not by the ear: `launch/engine/runtime/audio.js` uses noise, filters and envelopes only.

## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs
- Event `property.published` with `payload.tier = "Campaign"` → step `render_reel` (recipe condition `tiers: ["Campaign"]`, architecture 5). Heavy step: dispatched to `render.yml`.
- Job data (B9 Contract, B7 emits it, B8 passes it as `payload.data`): `{ property_id, slug, tier, market, submission_id?, revision? }`.
- Render spec, built only by `buildReelSpec(property, media)` in `src/server/assets/reel-spec.ts`, which lives under `src/server` so the job-runner's import map (`@mop/server/*`, B8) resolves it: `{ kind: "reel", property: { title, city, state, market, price, currency, beds, baths, interiorSqFt, type }, shots: [{ key, alt, orientation }] (4 photographs plus 6 tiles for the grid), copy: { location, title, facts, price, site, credit }, sound: { bed: "wind" | "water" | "room" | "city", seed: number } }`. It reads `properties` (`title`, `city`, `state`, `market_slug`, `price`, `currency`, `beds`, `baths`, `interior_sq_ft`, `type`, `campaign_tier`) and `property_media` (`variants`, `alt`, `orientation`, `sort_order`) for `data.property_id`. `meta.spec_hash = specHash(spec)` imported from B9's `src/server/assets/spec.ts`, which this slice does not change. `shots[].key` is the R2 key of the `hero` variant for a landscape photograph and of the `carousel` variant for a portrait one; the spec never carries a URL, the runner resolves keys to local files (see `scripts/render-reel.mjs`). Shot choice: media in `sort_order`; the first 4 are the camera shots and the next 6 are the grid tiles; with 4 to 9 photographs the tiles reuse the shots in order; fewer than 4 is refused by the step (invariant 9).
- `sound.bed` comes from `bedFor(type, market)` in `reel-spec.ts`, one exhaustive table (ASSUMED, tunable there): `Waterfront` in any market gets water; `Apartment` or `Penthouse` in `new-york` gets city; `Apartment` or `Penthouse` in `california` or `florida` gets room; `Estate`, `Residence`, `Townhouse` or `Farmhouse` in `new-york` gets room; the same four in `california` or `florida` get wind. `seed` = first 8 hex digits of the property id, so a re-render is byte-for-byte reproducible.
- `copy` comes from `reelCopy(property)` in `reel-spec.ts` and is every string the scene draws (invariant 5): `location` (city and state, uppercase), `title`, `facts` ("5 BED · 4.5 BATH · 4,320 SF"), `price`, `site` (`matterofplace.com`), `credit` ("A product of Omnikom", the start of B16's `t.footer.line` in `src/lib/strings.ts`, asserted by `copy.test.ts`).
- Photographs from R2 variants `hero` (landscape) or `carousel` (portrait), never the original. R2 is off until the operator enables it (S50, E8), so until then only the fixture spec with local image paths runs; every step that reads or writes R2 is marked BLOCKED below.

Outputs
- Asset `reel` (the row of `upsert_asset_stub(property_id, 'reel', revision, job_id)`) with `files = [{ role: "video", r2_key: "assets/<property_id>/reel/r<rev>/reel.<hash8>.mp4", w: 1080, h: 1920, bytes }, { role: "poster", r2_key: "assets/<property_id>/reel/r<rev>/poster.<hash8>.jpg", w: 1080, h: 1920, bytes }]`, keys from B9's `mediaKey()` in `scripts/lib/media-key.mjs` (content-hashed, F24, B9 invariant 8: a re-render is a new key, never an overwrite), uploaded with B9's `putIfMissing(key, body, contentType)` from `scripts/lib/r2.mjs` with `video/mp4` or `image/jpeg` and the immutable Cache-Control header; `meta = { duration_s, fps, gate: { coverage, longest_static_s, cuts, lufs, true_peak, flatness }, spec_hash }`; `caption`, `alt_text` and `meta.captions` are written by B9's `write_captions` only (it creates the reel stub itself for Campaign, B9 Files), so the order of the two steps does not matter.
- On approval: the trigger `assets_reel_video` (Data changes) calls `attach_reel`, which writes B2's existing `properties` columns `video_src` and `video_poster` (the R2 keys of the video and the poster, never URLs, A13), `video_caption` and `video_duration` (text, display form "0:18"). B3's `toProperty` turns them into the existing domain shape `PropertyVideo { src, poster, caption, duration }` (`src/domain/property.ts`) through `mediaUrl`, so the dossier gallery plays it. No column, domain field or contract changes, so G-004 is not engaged.
- Event: `asset.approved` (B9). Instagram publishing of the reel is B10's mapping row.
- Failure path: a gate failure or a size mismatch is reported as `{ status: "failed", error: "gate_failed: <check> <value>", retryable: false }`, so the job goes straight to `dead` (B8 Contract) and B9's trigger `assets_job_error` copies the error into `assets.render_error`; the asset stays `pending` and screen 10 shows Re-render. A render that exceeds its step timeout is reported the same way with `error: "reel_timeout"` (the script writes that provisional non-retryable result before it starts and overwrites it on completion). `r2_unavailable` (R2 off or an `R2_*` variable unset, F18), a failed download or a failed upload are `retryable: true` and follow B8's backoff (`max_attempts` 5). A runner lost without any callback is failed by B8's reaper after 30 minutes and retried under the same policy.

Invariants
1. Campaign only (S24): `render_reel` re-reads `properties.campaign_tier` (it does not trust `data.tier` alone) and throws `NonRetryableError("not_campaign")` for anything else, whatever the recipe says; an unpublished property throws `NonRetryableError("not_published")`, as B9's render steps do.
2. No music, no exceptions (S36). Sound is produced by `launch/engine/runtime/audio.js` from a cue list: noise beds and one-shots (air, room, wind, water, city, step, door, paper, fabric, whoosh, impact). No oscillator node exists in that module; a test greps for `createOscillator`, `OscillatorNode`, `Tone.` and fails on any hit. Rules from MOTION-BIBLE §3 hold: no pitched sustained tone, chord, melody, rhythmic loop or vocal; silence is a beat.
3. Moves come only from MOTION-BIBLE §2 (M1 to M13). Forbidden list applies: no bounce, elastic, spin, particles, lens flare, gradients, glow, counters, zoom-blur, stock wipes. Easing `power2.out` arrivals, `power3.inOut` camera, `expo.out` masks, `none` for camera moves on photographs (P-025), springs only on M10 (`back.out` with a strength of 1.3 or less, inside the scene's `gridAssemble` function and nowhere else). `tests/unit/reel/moves.test.ts` reads `launch/reel/scene.mjs` and fails on any other `ease:` value and on the text `bounce`, `elastic`, `rough`, `particle`, `gradient` or `glow`.
4. Quiet means palette, type and copy, never a static screen (CLAUDE.md, S37): the reel gate requires motion in at least 85 percent of frames and no static run above 1.0 second.
5. Copy on screen is calm and factual: wordmark, LOCATION (uppercase Jost, tracking), title (Cormorant), facts line ("5 BED · 4.5 BATH · 4,320 SF"), price, `matterofplace.com`, "A product of Omnikom" (S33 wording as B16 fixes it). No "JUST LISTED", no exclamation, no em dash, no guarantee. The scene draws only the strings in `spec.copy` (built by `reelCopy`); `tests/unit/reel/copy.test.ts` checks `reelCopy` output and the `copy` block of `launch/reel/fixture-spec.json`: no em dash (U+2014), no `!`, no `JUST LISTED`, and every string passes B9's `lintCaption(text, property, "instagram")`.
6. Deterministic render: no wall clock (the runtime seeks a paused GSAP master, `engine/runtime/film.js`), seeded noise, fixed frame rate. Same spec hash, same MP4 bytes up to encoder version. Proved twice: `reel-spec.test.ts` (same input gives the same `specHash`, a changed bed gives a different one) and step 4 (stills captured twice from the same spec have the same SHA-256).
7. Free tier (P-009): about 3 minutes of Actions per reel is a measured planning figure from the launch engine; the budget rule is at most 10 reels a month at launch (about 30 to 50 minutes of 2,000). It is a planning rule, not a code cap: Campaign is the only tier that renders a reel (invariant 1), the month's count is the screen 16 job list filtered on type `render_reel`, and the Actions minutes line of B14's `usage.mjs` (`collectors/github.mjs` against `limits.json`, 70 percent alarm) is the measurement. Frames are deleted on the runner as soon as the MP4 verifies (P-016).
8. The step never posts. Output is `pending` until approved (S23): `onResult` writes only `files`, `meta` and `render_error` (cleared), never `status`, `caption` or `alt_text`, and enqueues no job; `render-reel.test.ts` asserts the asset is still `pending` and no `post_meta`, `post_x` or `post_linkedin` job exists after `onResult`.
9. Variants first (B8b invariant 6, the same rule as B9 invariant 10): `render_reel.run` calls `upsert_asset_stub` first, throws `NonRetryableError("too_few_photos")` when the property has fewer than 4 `property_media` rows, then calls `variantsReady(db, property_id, "hero")` and `variantsReady(db, property_id, "carousel")` from B9's `spec.ts`; while either is false it returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: now + 2 minutes, reason: "variants_pending" }` (no attempt consumed). The wait is measured from the `created_at` of the reel stub, and after 60 minutes the step throws `NonRetryableError("variants_missing")`. Only then does it build the spec and call `dispatchHeavy` (B8) with `{ spec, spec_hash, revision }`.

Permissions: as B9 for the asset actions (media_ops, chief_editor). The reel player on screen 10 is read-only for other roles.

## Storyboard of the reel (the storyboard file states this table; timings are the design, the review loop may move them)
Format 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 18.0 s = 540 frames. Palette, type, copy fixed by CLAUDE.md; composition, rhythm and photo choice are the producer's call (MOTION-BIBLE §8).
| Beat | s | Picture | Moves | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0 to 3.0 | photograph 1 fills the frame from frame 0, in motion | M6 push-in 6 to 9 percent, M8 rack focus 8 px to 0 in the first second, M2 LOCATION label opens | bed (wind, water, room or city per rule) fades in |
| 2 | 3.0 to 3.7 | frame divides and reveals photograph 2 | M9 split slide 0.7 s | whoosh |
| 3 | 3.7 to 7.0 | photograph 2, title over it | M7 crane (vertical drift, rotation 1.5 degrees or less), M1 mask reveal of the title, M3 weight shift | bed continues, a stone step for interiors |
| 4 | 7.0 to 7.7 | split into photograph 3 | M9 | whoosh |
| 5 | 7.7 to 11.0 | photograph 3, facts row | M6, M4 line wipe draws under the facts, M1 masks the row in | paper crackle, quiet |
| 6 | 11.0 to 11.2 | Obsidian, 6 frames | M13 hard cut to black | whoosh into a silence beat of 0.5 s |
| 7 | 11.2 to 15.4 | six tiles assemble, one grows to full frame | M10 (1.4 s) then M6 on the full tile | fabric, whoosh, bed returns |
| 8 | 15.4 to 18.0 | last photograph moving underneath, price and `matterofplace.com` mask in, the wordmark locks | M1, M2, M5 fast, M11 light sweep once | impact (non-pitched, under 80 Hz) on the lock, room tone out |
Notes: one hold on a flat field never exceeds about 0.8 s and an end card always has a moving photograph under it (GOTCHAS P-025). Camera moves on photographs run at constant speed (`ease: "none"`) so the gate does not read eased-out moves as static. A landscape photograph in a 9:16 frame is scaled to about 1.2 times cover and centred; horizontal pan is not in the vocabulary and is not used.
