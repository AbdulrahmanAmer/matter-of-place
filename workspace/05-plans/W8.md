# W8: Instagram end to end, from a design to a reply

The operator, 2026-10-15: "the actual ability to create a post and write a proper caption and push those things online
to the Instagram account ... what are the tooling that are still missing". Found by reading the code and the live
database the same day: publishing is built (`src/server/channels/meta.ts`: single image, carousel, reel and story
through the Graph API, token refresh, half-failure recovery; per-post insights in `meta-metrics.ts`), and a published
property renders its own cover, carousel, story and reel. Live: 0 assets, 0 social posts, Instagram disabled.

Matter builds these through the merge gate, after W7 section E (reels), one pull request per item, the senior way:
extend the render steps, the assets table and the posting path that exist; never a second pipeline. A box is ticked
only when the thing works and was observed working.

## A. Something to post before the first listing

- [ ] 1. **Brand posts without a property.** `assets.property_id` is `not null`, so nothing but a listing can be
  designed. Make it nullable for a new subject column (`property | market | story | brand`), with the approve guard,
  RLS and retention following, and add brand templates to the render engine: a coming-soon post, a market note
  (California, New York, Florida), an editorial story card, a "hear about homes here" post that leads to the market's
  interest signup. The look follows the operator's post brief (`launch/`), the palette and type of CLAUDE.md, no
  gradients, glow or gold; stills pass a visual review, reels pass the motion gate. Proof: one of each rendered, in
  Visuals, pending.
- [ ] 2. **A Social proposal becomes a real draft.** Today an approved `social_post` proposal records the decision
  and nothing else (`workforce_decide`). Approved, it renders the chosen template (or asks for a `reel`, W7 item 19),
  attaches the caption as written, and puts it in Visuals as a pending asset; approving that asset posts it through
  the path that exists (`asset.approved` recipe, `post_meta`). Proof: a Social proposal approved, its asset rendered,
  approved, and (once Instagram is on) posted.

## B. Captions without a person running them

- [ ] 3. **The captions runner on a schedule.** `write_captions` jobs are written only by `bun run captions` by hand
  (`scripts/captions-runner.ts`). A scheduled task "MOP Captions" runs it every 10 minutes like the other MOP tasks
  (Priority 5, full paths, P-3457, P-3461), and the health check names a caption job waiting more than an hour.
  Proof: a property published on mop-dev gets its captions without anyone running a command.

## C. Talking back

- [ ] 4. **Comments and messages.** Nothing reads Instagram comments or direct messages. Read them on a schedule
  (Graph API comments and conversations, with the permissions the Meta app asks for), show them on a screen beside
  the post, wake the Social colleague on a new one, and send its replies through Approvals (decided by Matter). Proof:
  a comment on a test post answered from Approvals.
- [ ] 5. **Account numbers.** Per-post insights are fetched; followers, profile visits, reach and impressions over time
  are not. Fetch the account's insights daily into the analytics tables, show them in Reports, and give the Analyst
  and the Social colleague the week's numbers in their briefing. Proof: a week of account numbers on the screen.
