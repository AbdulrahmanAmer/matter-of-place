What each step reads from the asset is fixed in two pure functions in `src/server/channels/index.ts`, tested by `tests/unit/channels/targets.test.ts`:
- `targetsFor(kind, settings)` returns the target channels of a kind from the table above (carousel: `instagram`, plus `linkedin` when `settings.linkedin.multi_image` is true; cover: `x`, `facebook`, plus `linkedin` when it is not; story: `instagram`, `facebook`; reel: `instagram`). Outputs
- `social_posts` rows (architecture 3.6): `asset_id`, `channel`, `status` `scheduled|posted|failed`, `scheduled_at`, `posted_at`, `remote_id`, `permalink`, `metrics jsonb`, `error`. Unique `(asset_id, channel)`. No status is added: a cancelled post is `failed` with `error = 'cancelled'`; in-flight markers live in `error` (`container:<id>` for Meta, `inflight:<UTC timestamp>:media:<media_id>` for X and `inflight:<UTC timestamp>:image:<image URN>` for LinkedIn, INT-01) while the row is `scheduled`; a crash whose outcome cannot be read back is `failed` with `error = 'outcome_unknown'`.
- After a successful publish the asset becomes `published` (architecture 3.6 asset statuses) once every enabled target channel of `targetsFor(kind, settings)` has a `posted` row. This happens inside the SQL function `mark_social_post_posted` (Data changes), which locks the asset row, so two channels finishing at once cannot both miss the last one.
- The last two submission states are this slice's (G60). A submission completes when its `campaigns.ends_on` has passed, and when it has no campaign or `ends_on` is still empty, 30 days after `published_at`: `complete_distributed_submissions` applies both cases, and its database test has one row for each. Campaign dates (DL-09, ruling H17): B6 inserts `starts_on` and `ends_on` empty, and on the first publish B7's `publish_property` sets `starts_on` to the publish date in the market's zone and `ends_on = starts_on + <window> - 1`, where the window in days per product is a `settings` value edited in `/admin` (null for a product whose window the exposure page does not state, which leaves `ends_on` null and the 30-day rule in force); a republish does not move them. The rule applies to a `Published` submission as well as a `Distribution Active` one (B2's `submission_transition_allowed` gains `Published` to `Completed`, DL-09), so a property published with no post (accounts missing, every asset rejected) still completes. `Published` becomes `Distribution Active` when the first `social_posts` row of the property reaches `posted`: `mark_social_post_posted`, the transaction that stores the remote id, moves the property's submission (`properties.submission_id`) from `Published` to `Distribution Active`, guarded by B2's `submission_transition_allowed` and checked again by its `enforce_editorial_gate` trigger; a later posted row, or a property without a submission, changes nothing. `Distribution Active` or `Published` becomes `Completed` in the daily part of the reconcile job (`reconcile-social.ts`) through `complete_distributed_submissions` (Data changes): a submission with a `campaigns` row once that row's `ends_on` has passed, otherwise 30 days after the property's `published_at`. No event is emitted for either move.
- `metrics jsonb` normalised to `{ reach, views, saves, shares, likes, comments, clicks, fetched_at, raw }`; `raw` keeps the platform's answer untouched so a renamed metric loses nothing. Per channel: Instagram from Graph insights; X from the post's `public_metrics` (impressions only where the tier returns them, UNPROVEN) mapped to views, likes, shares (reposts), comments (replies); LinkedIn from the organisation share statistics (impressions, clicks, likes, comments, shares; UNPROVEN which are available to our app). Fields a channel does not return stay null, never zero.
- Rows in `campaign_reports` (sketch columns: `campaign_id, period_start, period_end, impressions, reach, clicks, video_views, ctr, geography, channel_mix, owned_distribution, top_creative`), one per campaign per ISO week, filled by `buildCampaignReport`; this slice's migration adds the unique constraint `(campaign_id, period_start)` (B2 creates the table without it).
- Channel health for screens 2 and 12: last post, last error, token expiry from `settings.meta.token_expires_at` (B8's `meta_token_refresh` job stores it from `GET /debug_token`; null means the token never expires) and from `settings.x` and `settings.linkedin` (`token_expires_at`, `token_checked_at`, `token_state` `ok|dead|version_expired`, written through `record_channel_check` once a day by the social part of the reconcile job and by the `--check` mode of the authorize scripts), with the days left shown and amber at 14 or fewer, red at 7 or fewer (GS-01). Meta's validity is stored too (INT-06): B8's `meta_token_refresh` reads `data.is_valid`, `data.scopes` and `data.data_access_expires_at` from `debug_token` and its `meta_token_record` writes `token_state` (`ok`, `dead` or `scopes_missing`), `missing_scopes` and `data_access_expires_at` into `settings.meta`, the field names `settings.x` and `settings.linkedin` already use; `tokenHealth` treats any `token_state` other than `ok` as red, and a `data_access_expires_at` within 14 days as amber. A dead token is red whatever the expiry says: `channelHealth` sets `level: "red"` for a channel whose `settings.<channel>.token_state` is `dead` (or, for Meta, `scopes_missing`), or whose newest `failed` row has `error = 'token_dead'` with no later `posted` row (Meta code 190 writes that row error; the next good post or check clears it); `version_expired` (LinkedIn, Risks) is amber with the label "API version expired". `channelHealth` also returns for `x` the month's read count `settings.x.usage.reads` against `settings.x.read_allowance`, amber at 70 percent (invariant 11, P-009). Served by `channelHealth(db)` behind `GET /api/admin/channels/health`, which reads `settings` and `social_posts` only. Screen 12 shows it in `ChannelCards.tsx`; screen 2 shows it in `src/admin/dashboard/ChannelHealthTile.tsx` (Files).
- The token itself comes from `getMetaToken(db)` (`src/server/channels/meta-token.ts`): the Vault secret `meta_page_token` read with B8's `get_vault_secret('meta_page_token')` when B8's refresh job has written one, otherwise the `META_PAGE_TOKEN` env secret. The Graph client never reads the env directly.

Invariants
1. Each platform API is called only from its own step (`post_meta`, `post_x`, `post_linkedin`) and the reconcile job, never from a request (architecture 1). The admin functions never call a platform: `refreshMetrics(postId)` calls the one SQL function `refresh_social_post_metrics` (Data changes), which enqueues the system job `reconcile` with params `{ post_ids: [postId] }` through B8's `enqueue_job_manual('reconcile', <post_id>, ...)` (key `reconcile:<post_id>:<n>`, B8 invariant 1, `n` computed by B8 under its advisory lock) and writes the audit row `channels.metrics_refresh` in the same transaction, and answers 202; `channelHealth` reads `settings` and `social_posts` only. Every Graph call carries `appsecret_proof` (HMAC-SHA256 of the token with `META_APP_SECRET`, hex), computed with `hmacSha256` and `toHex` from B1b's `src/server/lib/crypto.ts`; no file of this slice calls `crypto.subtle` itself (CS-04).
2. Exactly-once posting, all inside `postToChannel` (`src/server/channels/post-to-channel.ts`), the one helper the three post steps share. On entry it reads the `social_posts` row: `posted` ends the step; `failed` with `cancelled` ends it with `skipped: cancelled`. The row moves to `posted` in the same transaction that stores `remote_id` (`mark_social_post_posted`). In-flight markers make a crash safe on every channel, and each names a provider handle that exists before the publishing call, so recovery never matches on caption text (INT-01: X returns the property link rewritten to t.co and LinkedIn returns its commentary escaped): Meta stores `container:<id>` in `social_posts.error` as soon as the container exists; X uploads the media first and stores `inflight:<UTC timestamp>:media:<media_id>`; LinkedIn runs `initializeUpload` and the byte upload first and stores `inflight:<UTC timestamp>:image:<image URN>`; X and LinkedIn write the marker just before the create call. Every marker is written with `set_social_post_inflight(p_id, p_marker)`, a compare-and-set that returns false when the row already holds a marker (Data changes): another job holds the row (for example `meta-test-post.ts` beside the recipe's `post_meta`, step 7), so the step returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: now + 2 minutes, reason: "in_flight_elsewhere" }` and calls nothing. A run that finds an `inflight:` marker younger than 2 minutes returns the same `retry_at`, because the run that wrote it may still be between its create call and `mark_social_post_posted`. A run that finds an older marker resolves it before anything else, through the adapter's `findRecentPost(marker, since = scheduled_at)`: X reads `GET https://api.x.com/2/users/:id/tweets?start_time=<scheduled_at>&expansions=attachments.media_keys` and adopts the tweet whose `attachments.media_keys` contains `3_<media_id>`; LinkedIn reads `GET https://api.linkedin.com/rest/posts?q=author&author=<organization_urn>` and adopts the post whose `content.media.id`, or one of whose `content.multiImage.images[].id`, equals the stored URN; Meta polls the stored container with `waitForContainer`, calls `media_publish` once on `FINISHED` (the container was never published) and on `PUBLISHED` adopts the newest media of `GET /{ig-user-id}/media?fields=caption,timestamp,permalink` whose `timestamp` is at or after `scheduled_at` (Instagram keeps captions verbatim, so a caption check stays valid for Meta only). An adopted post is marked `posted` with no create call. When the X or LinkedIn lookup errors, is refused (a 4xx or a 429), finds nothing, or cannot run because the X read budget is spent (invariant 11, ruling H20), the step never posts again by itself: it fails the row with `error = 'outcome_unknown'` through `fail_social_post` and sends the `social_failed` `notifyAdmin` of invariant 3b; a human checks the feed and uses Retry with `force: true` on screen 12, which clears the marker. `fail_social_post` changes only a `scheduled` row, so a run that loses a race never fails a row another run posted. Meta containers, reels above all, can stay `IN_PROGRESS` for minutes: when `waitForContainer` still answers `IN_PROGRESS` after its 20 seconds, the adapter returns `in_progress`, `postToChannel` keeps `container:<id>` in `social_posts.error` and the step returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: now + 2 minutes, reason: "container_in_progress" }` (B8 invariant 3: no attempt consumed), and the next run polls the same container again; `ERROR` or `EXPIRED` clears the marker with `set_social_post_inflight(p_id, null)` and creates a new container in the same run; a container still `IN_PROGRESS` 60 minutes after the row's `scheduled_at` fails the row with `container_timeout` through `fail_social_post` and sends the failed-post `notifyAdmin` of invariant 3b. Because B8 retries a thrown job after 30 seconds, this lookup happens on the retry itself (after the 2-minute wait), not a day later; the reconcile job applies the same rule to a marker older than one hour: it adopts a match or records `outcome_unknown`, and it never publishes. Across revisions: a re-render is a new asset row (B9), so `postToChannel` also fails the row with `revision_already_posted` when another asset with the same `(property_id, kind)` already has a `posted` row on this channel; a deliberate repost is Retry on screen 12 with `force: true`, which skips this check and is audited.
3. Outside the posting window the step returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: <next slot> }` (B8 invariant 3: no attempt consumed). The asset stays approved, the row stays `scheduled` with `scheduled_at`. `nextWindowSlot(now, window)` is a pure function that respects `tz` and daylight-saving changes and the `daily_cap`.

Because `approval_mode` lives on each channel row and an asset is approved once, an asset counts as `auto` only when the function returns `auto` for every enabled target channel of `targetsFor(kind, settings)`. `mayApprove(actor, tier, channelRows, today)` returns true for humans and, for `actor_kind = agent`, only in that case; B9's `approveAsset` calls it (403 `human_approval_required` otherwise) and, for an agent it allows, passes the evidence `{ tier, channels, auto_after }` to `approve_asset`. Everything else in `approve_asset` (status, `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `og_image_key`, the `asset.approved` event in the same transaction, G20) is unchanged. `postToChannel` re-checks on every run: when the asset's `approved_by` is null or belongs to a `user_roles` row with `actor_kind = agent`, and `effectiveApprovalMode` for this channel is not `auto`, the row becomes `failed` with `human_approval_required` and nothing is posted. Seeds: B8b's (`Feature`, `Reach` and `Campaign` all `manual`, `auto_after` null, which `effectiveApprovalMode` reads as manual everywhere); this slice does not touch `approval_mode`. B7's agent daily cap counts only `submissions.decline` and `submissions.accept` audit rows (G27), so auto approvals do not consume it.
4a. What starts an auto approval: the last line of B9's `onResult` in `render-cover.ts`, `render-carousel.ts` and `render-story.ts` calls `maybeAutoApprove` for the asset it just updated, and the end of `write-captions.ts`'s `run` calls it once for each of the `cover`, `carousel` and `story` assets it wrote in that run (`newsletter_block`, `standalone_email` and `reel` are never auto-approved: no social target, or Campaign only) (listed under Change). It approves only when the asset is `pending`, `files`, `caption` and `alt_text` are set (the same rule as B9's `assets_approve_guard`), `meta.caption_lint` is not `failed`, the kind has at least one enabled target channel, and the mode is `auto` as defined in invariant 4; otherwise it returns `not_eligible` with the reason (`not_pending`, `incomplete`, `lint_failed`, `no_target`, `tier_manual`) and changes nothing. A second call for an approved asset does nothing. Reels are Campaign tier only (S24) and Campaign never goes automatic, so `render-reel.ts` (B12) needs no call.
3a. Failures inside the window (GG-04). A retryable Graph error (classes `retryable` and `retry_at` of `meta-errors.ts`) is retried inside the current window while it lasts: the step throws a retryable error and B8's backoff (30 s, 1 minute, 2 minutes, ...) runs the job again. `planRetry(now, window, alreadyPostedToday, attempt, maxAttempts)` in `window.ts` (pure, zone and daylight-saving aware; `attempt` and `maxAttempts` are the job's `attempts` and `max_attempts`, which is 12 for `post_meta`, `post_x` and `post_linkedin` from the `maxAttempts` of B8b's step specs, about 5 hours of B8 backoff before dead, E2E-03) decides per failure: if the next backoff time still falls inside the window and the daily cap has room, retry there; otherwise, or on the last attempt, the step returns `{ status: "retry_at", at: <next window slot>, reason: "window_moved" }` so the job is not dead-lettered and no attempt is consumed. Each move writes an `audit_log` row `channels.reschedule` (`actor_id` null, `actor_kind` null, G43) with a note ("Instagram answered with a temporary error, moved to Tue 09:00 PT"), which screen 12 shows under the row through `entityTimeline`; `scheduled_at` is updated. After 3 moves for the same post (count of those audit rows, ASSUMED limit) the row becomes `failed` with the last error, red on screen 12 with Retry, and `notify_admin` is enqueued once. `non_retryable` errors and `token_dead` never move: they fail at once (invariant 3b).
3b. Every failure is visible: a failed row shows red on screen 12 with Retry; a token error (Graph code 190, or `invalid_grant` from the X or LinkedIn refresh) writes the row's `error = 'token_dead'`, which turns channel health red on screens 2 and 12 (Outputs, channel health), and sends `notify_admin`. ## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- G-006: `META_*` server side only; G-011: no scheduler, no paid Meta product; P-009: the social part adds one daily job and a few platform calls a day to B8's 15-minute `reconcile` run; the other ticks only make one idempotent enqueue, negligible.
- Free tier and Meta limits: Instagram content publishing has a rolling 24-hour cap per account (the number, currently believed 100, is UNPROVEN and is read live from `content_publishing_limit`); our own cap of 2 per channel per day sits far below it. Graph app-level and business-use-case throttles come back as codes 4, 17, 32, 613 and 80002 and must map to `retry_at`, never to `failed`.
- Meta fetches images itself. Instagram rejects some hosts and any URL that redirects or is slow; test the real `mediaUrl(key)` URL (prefix `MEDIA_BASE_URL`) in step 7 (BLOCKED while R2 is off, E8). If R2 public access is blocked by a Cloudflare rule (bot fight mode, hotlink protection) the container fails with a media error that looks like a Meta bug.
- Carousels: images must share one aspect ratio (Instagram crops to the first); B9 produces 1080×1350 for every slide for that reason.
- Instagram rules for Stories: image stories are 9:16, 1080×1920; a story is not a feed post and does not show in the grid; metrics for stories are only available for a limited window, hence the 20-hour pull.
- Insights metrics were renamed and removed several times (impressions and others). Behaviour depends on the app's Graph version and post type; the mapping file plus stored `raw` is the guard. UNPROVEN until step 3.
- Instagram Login versus Facebook Login: this plan uses Facebook Login because one Page token covers Instagram and the later Facebook block. If Meta forces the Instagram-Login variant, tokens differ (per Instagram account, 60-day, refreshable) and `health()` must refresh them; the interface does not change.
- X (UNPROVEN): the free tier's write limits and whether media upload is included are measured in step 3a, not assumed; the API's pricing and tiers have changed more than once, so re-read them on the day. Access tokens are short-lived (about two hours, UNPROVEN), so `getChannelToken` refreshes on demand before a post and stores the new refresh token in Vault each time through `store_channel_token`, whose advisory lock and refresh-hash compare stop two runs from overwriting each other (G21), because a rotated refresh token used twice is refused (a lost refresh token means a manual re-consent with `scripts/x-authorize.ts`). The daily `checkChannelToken` writes `settings.x` once a day. Token values never go to `settings`: they are secrets and live in Vault, and `put_channel_ids` refuses any field outside the id list; the keys `meta`, `x` and `linkedin` do not bump `catalog_version` (G21).
- LinkedIn (UNPROVEN): product approval is the schedule risk, and a rejected application would leave two live channels. Posting versions are dated headers that expire after about a year, so `settings.linkedin.api_version` is pinned and checked every day by `checkChannelToken('linkedin')` in the social part of the reconcile job: a 426 or `VERSION_MISSING` or `NONEXISTENT_VERSION` answer stores `token_state = 'version_expired'`, the card turns amber with "API version expired", and the fix is a new `api_version` through the Account ids form after reading the changelog. Before that happens, B8's health check `linkedin_version` (`src/server/jobs/system/health/providers.ts`, INT-07) warns once `settings.linkedin.api_version` (YYYYMM) is 10 months old, so the version is renewed before LinkedIn sunsets it. Image posts need the upload step first; plain text or link posts are the fallback if multi-image is not available.
- Tone differs per channel: the X caption is short and the LinkedIn caption longer and aimed at agents and brokerages, both under the brand voice rules (B9 `write_captions`); neither claims leads or sales.
- GG-04: a moved post keeps its approval and its place in `social_posts`; it is never re-created, so the exactly-once rule (invariant 2) still holds. The 3-move limit is ASSUMED; a channel that fails for a day should turn red, not silently slide.
- GS-01: token expiry is owned by B8's `meta_token_refresh` job; this slice only reads its result and raises the screen state. If the owner uses the System User route (step 2), that token does not expire and `token_expires_at` stays null; the health card then shows "never" and the job only checks that the token is still valid, which it now reads from `debug_token`'s `is_valid` and `scopes` (INT-06), so a revoked or under-scoped never-expiring token turns red instead of showing "never". Until the owner finishes step 1 the job is `skipped: not_configured` and the health card says "not connected".
- Development-mode limits: the app works only for roles on the app. If ever the app must act for a client's account (it will not, no client posting in v1), Advanced access is required.
- P-008 style trap in tests: never type a JSON payload with backslashes inline in Bash; use files.
- Deno imports (CS-01): the job runner loads the post steps and, through them, `src/server/channels/**`, `src/server/reports/build.ts` and `src/server/lib/runtime-env.ts`; every relative, `@/server/` or `@/domain/` import in those files carries the `.ts` extension, or the `deno` step of the CI `check` job (`deno check` of `supabase/functions/job-runner/index.ts`, B3 adds the step, B8 points it at the runner) goes red.
- X reads (INT-08, ruling H20): the free tier's read allowance may be near zero (UNPROVEN until step 3a); the budget of invariant 11 keeps the in-flight lookup alive, and with no allowance X metrics leave the reports and an X crash goes to a human as `outcome_unknown`. No paid X tier is bought.
- Supabase free projects pause after 7 idle days; a paused project stops the runner and therefore posting. B8b's `keepwarm` covers it.
