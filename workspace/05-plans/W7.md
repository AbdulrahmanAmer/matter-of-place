# W7: what has no screen, and what the staff cannot see

The operator, 2026-10-15: "what else did we forget to build and doesn't have a screen or the employees don't have the
ability to see ... create a list and hand those to matter when he is ready to actually work on building". Found by
mapping the 63 tables of the live database against the 31 admin screens and against what a colleague's briefing holds
(three counts, our site facts since PR 378, its own open approvals, its lessons, the knowledge base).

Matter builds these through the merge gate, one pull request per item, in the order below; the orchestrator gates and
reviews. A box is ticked only when the thing works and was observed working. Items marked PERSON need the operator's
hands and are listed so nothing is forgotten, not for Matter.

## A. Things that happen and no screen shows

- [ ] 1. **Sent email log.** Every email we sent (`email_messages`), to whom, its template, and what became of it
  (`email_events`: delivered, opened, bounced, complained), plus the suppression list (`email_suppressions`). Today a
  bounce on a client's address is invisible. Screen under System (or a tab on the person's page in People).
- [ ] 2. **Email replies inbox.** The replies W4 reads from the mailbox (`email_replies`) reach a colleague's thread
  only. A screen to read each reply beside the email it answers, see who owns it, and mark it answered or ignored.
- [ ] 3. **Campaigns.** The `campaigns` table has no screen: the Campaigns colleague drafts plans into Approvals, but
  an approved plan has nowhere to live. A list of campaigns with state, market and budget, linked to its report
  (Reports already reads `campaign_reports`).
- [ ] 4. **The team's memory.** The daily meeting's decisions (`agent_decisions`), the lessons each colleague was
  taught (`agent_lessons`) and the history of their instructions (`agent_instruction_versions`) are written and never
  shown. Tabs in Office: Decisions (open, done, missed), Lessons, Instruction history. W5 item 14 is observed here.
- [ ] 5. **Events that did not run.** `events` and `event_fanout_failures` (an event no recipe could plan) are only in
  the health check. A panel in System > Jobs: the last events, which recipe took each, and any that failed to plan.
- [ ] 6. **Redirects.** `redirects` and `slug_history` (old addresses of renamed properties) have no screen; a renamed
  page's old link can only be checked by hand. A read-only list under Settings, with a test-a-link box.
- [ ] 7. **Service usage.** `vendor_usage` (Workers requests, Sentry errors, Actions minutes) feeds one health check.
  Show the gauges with their free-plan limits under Settings, beside the Claude allowance of W6 item 1.

## B. Things the staff cannot see (their briefing lacks it)

  this week and in total (the same read as the Interest screen, counts only, no addresses).
- [ ] 9. **The content of new work**, not only its count: the newest submissions and inquiries in short form (market,
  type, price band, state) for the Editor and the Concierge, so a briefing can name the request it is about.
- [ ] 10. **Site numbers** for the Analyst: `analytics_daily` for the last 7 and 28 days (visits, top pages, signups,
  inquiries by market), so its weekly report reads real numbers instead of searching for them.
- [ ] 11. **What happened to their proposals**: approved, declined (with the reason) or edited before sending, for
  every colleague, not only the declines that became lessons.
- [ ] 12. **Email delivery** for the Concierge: whether its approved replies were delivered, opened or bounced
  (item 1's data).
- [ ] 13. **Their own usage**: W6 item 1 (usage-aware staff) already plans this; listed so it is not built twice.
