## Goal and observed exit
Money is a box with two doors (tech-stack 2, S32): acceptance never depends on payment, activation does. An accepted submission gets one numbered invoice from `/admin`, a PDF made in the job runner with `pdf-lib`, emailed to the agent through the seeded recipe; an admin marks it paid (or waives it) and activates the agent, which moves the submission to `Scheduled` and creates the property draft. Stripe can later write the same row through the same functions (S14 is superseded by S32 for launch).
Observed exit (completion map B6): "an admin issues an invoice and activates a test agent". Proven as `bunx playwright test tests/e2e/admin-invoice.spec.ts` against the dev server wired to `mop-dev` (no Docker, S50): an accepted test request gets the next invoice number (the spec reads `invoice_counters.last` for the current UTC year before issuing and expects `MOP-<year>-<last + 1, four digits>`, because `mop-dev` is shared and earlier runs have used numbers), mark paid then activate leaves the submission `Scheduled` with a property draft, and issuing on an unaccepted request is refused at the UI, the server function and the database. Two assertions of that spec depend on things that do not exist today and are BLOCKED until they do: the PDF object in R2 (BLOCKED until the operator turns R2 on and a bucket exists, E8) and the email with the PDF at a test inbox (BLOCKED until a Resend account and verified sending domain exist, E9). The PDF itself is rendered and checked locally without R2.

Screen 24 has "invoice numbering prefix" (admin-screens 24); terms, due days and methods are the A4 owner inputs (bank, wire, card by phone). Seed: prefix `MOP`, `due_days` 14 (matches the 14-day overdue highlight of screen 5), `terms` "Payable within 14 days of the invoice date." (ASSUMED wording), three methods `bank_transfer`, `wire`, `card_by_phone` with EMPTY instructions, `tax_line` "No sales tax is charged on this invoice." (ASSUMED: the product is a service and no US sales tax is applied unless the accountant or lawyer says otherwise; a placeholder line, nothing is computed), `late_terms` "Payment is due by the date shown. Overdue amounts may be subject to a late charge." (ASSUMED wording with no rate invented; lawyer review is a HARDEN gate).

Outputs

Additions made by this slice (architecture 3.4 already lists `due_at`, `paid_method`, `paid_reference`, `waived_by`, `invoice_snapshot`, the `void` status and `invoice_counters`; `created_at` and `updated_at` come from B2): `due_at timestamptz` (issue time plus `due_days`, stored so a later settings change does not move old invoices), `paid_method text`, `paid_reference text` (screen 6 "Mark paid (date, method, reference)"), `waived_by uuid`, `invoice_snapshot jsonb` (frozen at issue so a re-render years later is identical; GP-06 fields: `entity`, `address`, `contact`, `invoice_number`, `issue_date`, `due_date`, `description` (product name and its `line`), `amount`, `currency`, `tax_line`, `instructions`, `terms`, `late_terms`, plus the bill-to block). `buildInvoiceSnapshot` fills every field except `invoice_number`, `issue_date` and `due_date` (the constant `sqlAssignedFields` in `src/domain/payments.ts`); `issue_invoice` reads `settings.invoice->>'due_days'`, sets `issued_at = now()` and `due_at = now() + make_interval(days => due_days)`, assigns the number and stores `p_snapshot || jsonb_build_object('invoice_number', <number>, 'issue_date', <issued_at UTC date>, 'due_date', <due_at UTC date>)`, so the stored snapshot is complete. B2 (`20261001090600_commercial.sql`) already creates `payments`, `campaigns` (with `payment_id`) and `campaign_reports`; this slice ALTERS `payments` to add the columns marked ASSUMED below.
- `invoice_number` = `<prefix>-<YYYY>-<NNNN>`, gapless per UTC year (ASSUMED UTC). Architecture says "from a sequence"; a Postgres sequence leaves gaps on rollback and does not reset yearly, so the function uses `invoice_counters(year, last)` with `insert ... on conflict (year) do update set last = invoice_counters.last + 1 returning last`, inside the issuing transaction. B2 creates no sequence and no `next_invoice_number` function (B2 `20261001090600_commercial.sql`: `invoice_number` nullable, no default); this slice CREATES `next_invoice_number(p_prefix text default 'MOP')` once, with the counter table, so there is no older signature to drop or overload. `issue_invoice` passes `settings.invoice->>'prefix'`. Numbering per year is ASSUMED from the `MOP-YYYY-NNNN` format in architecture 3.4.
- PDF: US Letter, one page, `pdf-lib` standard fonts (Times Roman headings, Helvetica text; ASSUMED, brand fonts are a follow-up), colours from `theme.gen.ts` (B5). Stored in R2 at `invoices/<year>/<invoice_number>.pdf` in a separate private bucket, never in the media bucket (the media buckets are public: `mop-media-dev` through its `r2.dev` URL and `mop-media` through `media.matterofplace.com`, B2, and invoice keys are guessable). Bucket `mop-invoices-dev` for `mop-dev` and `mop-invoices` for `mop-prod` (ASSUMED names following B1b's media pair), no `r2.dev` URL and no custom domain, named by the environment variable `R2_INVOICES_BUCKET`; created with `bunx wrangler r2 bucket create mop-invoices-dev` (and `mop-invoices` at launch) with the admin token from `.env`, BLOCKED until the operator turns R2 on (E8). Every invoice read and write passes `R2_INVOICES_BUCKET` to `r2-s3.ts`; nothing invoice related uses `R2_BUCKET`. Content: wordmark and "A product of Omnikom."; invoice number, issue date, due date; From (entity, address, contact email and phone from `settings.site`); Bill to (agent name, brokerage, email); Property (address, city, state, zip); the description line (product name and its `line` from `exposure.ts`) with its amount; the tax line from the snapshot (`tax_line`, a placeholder, no tax computed); total with the currency code `USD`; payment terms and the late terms; preferred payment method and the instructions of every listed method; closing line "Editorial acceptance precedes this invoice." (ASSUMED wording).
- Events (all existing catalog types, no new one): `invoice.issued` payload `{ submission_id, payment_id, invoice_number, tier }`; `payment.marked` payload `{ submission_id, payment_id, status: "paid"|"waived", amount }` (waive reuses this type; there is no `payment.waived` in the catalog); `submission.activated` payload `{ submission_id, property_id, tier, market }`; `invoice.voided` payload `{ submission_id, payment_id, invoice_number, reason }`. Each is emitted by its SQL function through `emit_event` in the same transaction as the state change (`issue_invoice` emits `invoice.issued`, `mark_payment_paid` and `waive_payment` emit `payment.marked`, `void_payment` emits `invoice.voided`, `activate_submission` emits `submission.activated` once, never on the idempotent second call), and each function returns the new `event_id`. After the RPC returns, the service calls `fanoutEvent(db, event_id)` (B8b `src/server/automation/fanout.ts`) best effort, logging and ignoring its error; the runner's sweep is the safety net (B8b invariant 4). Recipes then send the invoice email (`send_email` template `invoice`, `attach: invoice_pdf`) and notify admins (B8b seeds `notify_admin` for `payment.marked` and `invoice.voided`).
- Submission states moved: `Accepted` to `Invoice Issued` (issue), `Invoice Issued` to `Scheduled` (activate). The state name is `Invoice Issued` (architecture 3.3); B2 corrected `submissionStates` in `src/domain/contracts.ts` to `Invoice Issued` (B2 step 11 proves no `Awaiting Payment` remains) and B7 step 4 verifies it.

Invariants
1. Acceptance before invoice, in three layers: the button is disabled in the UI; the server function refuses with 409 `not_accepted`; B2's trigger `enforce_editorial_gate` (migration `20261001090700_editorial_gate.sql`) raises for `Invoice Issued` and later states when `accepted_at` is null, and for `Scheduled` and later when no `payments` row for the submission has status `paid` or `waived` (architecture 3.4). B6 does not redefine the trigger; `tests/db/payments.db.test.ts` re-asserts it through the real functions so a regression in B2 shows here. B2's transition graph is mirrored in `src/domain/workflow.ts` (`canTransition`), which the service uses.
2. One live payment per submission: partial unique index on `payments(submission_id) where status in ('due','paid','waived')`.
3. Every state change is one SQL function (`issue_invoice`, `mark_payment_paid`, `waive_payment`, `void_payment`, `activate_submission`) that updates the row, writes `audit_log` through `write_audit` and calls `emit_event` in the same transaction (supabase-js has no multi-statement transactions; the TS server function does actor, authz, Zod and then one RPC).
4. The invoice number is assigned in the issuing transaction and never changes. A rolled-back issue consumes no number.
5. The invoice is frozen: the PDF is rendered from `payments.invoice_snapshot`, not from live settings.
6. Nothing external in a request: `issue_invoice` inserts the rows and enqueues the `invoice_pdf` job in its own transaction (`perform enqueue_job('invoice_pdf', jsonb_build_object('params', '{}'::jsonb, 'data', jsonb_build_object('payment_id', <id>)), 'invoice_pdf:' || <id>)`, B8's function and job envelope), so a committed invoice always has its PDF job and a rolled-back one has none; the service never calls `enqueueJob`. The runner renders, uploads and emails. `service.test.ts` runs every action with a `fetch` stub that throws, so any outside call from a request fails the test.
7. Issuing requires readiness: `settings.site` has `legal.entity`, `legal.address` and `contact.email`, and `settings.invoice.payment_methods` has at least one method with non-empty instructions. Also `settings.invoice.terms`, `late_terms` and `tax_line` must be non-empty (seeded, so this fails only if someone blanks them). Otherwise 409 `invoice_not_ready` with the missing list, and the screen shows it (no invented address, S33).
8. Issuing is allowed from `Accepted`, and from `Invoice Issued` only when no live payment exists (after a void). Activation requires status `paid` or `waived`, submission state `Invoice Issued`; it sets `activated_by` and `activated_at`, creates the property draft with `create_property_from_submission` when `property_id` is null, inserts one `campaigns` row when none exists for the payment (`property_id`, `submission_id`, `payment_id`, `package` = `payments.product`, `media_budget` = `coalesce(submissions.media_budget, 0)`, `starts_on` and `ends_on` null, which B10 reads as open bounds; ASSUMED: activation is the moment a campaign starts to exist, and no other plan writes `campaigns`), and is idempotent (a second call returns the same result, no second property, no second campaign, no second event).
9. Stripe is a stub interface only. Registry `adapters/index.ts` contains only `invoice_manual`; `stripe.ts` implements the interface with methods that throw `NotImplementedError` (exported by `adapters/types.ts`). When Stripe arrives it adds a webhook route that verifies the signature and calls `mark_payment_paid` with a null actor and `paid_method = 'stripe'`; the state machine and screens do not change.
10. Legal fields (GP-06, architecture 10): an issued invoice shows entity, address, invoice number, issue date, due date, description, amount, currency, a tax line, payment instructions and late terms, in the PDF and in the admin preview. The list is one constant, `requiredInvoiceFields`, in `src/domain/payments.ts`; `invoice-layout.test.ts` fails when `layoutInvoice` omits any of them for a complete snapshot, and `buildInvoiceSnapshot` throws when a required value is missing. Tax is a placeholder line only: nothing is computed, and nothing in the code names a rate.
11. Print (GQ-06): the admin invoice pages print cleanly on US Letter. `src/styles/admin/invoices.css` has an `@media print` block that hides every admin shell element marked `data-print="hide"` (ASSUMED attribute that B7's shell puts on navigation, toolbars and buttons), shows the preview at full width in `--foreground` on `--background` with no shadows or fixed positioning, sets `@page { size: letter; margin: 0.75in; }` (literal lengths, because `@page` cannot read custom properties; ASSUMED acceptable under G-007 since no colour is involved), and keeps the invoice block on one page (`break-inside: avoid`). The PDF stays the official copy; printing the preview is a convenience and is labelled Preview.

Void (A3): an invoice issued in error is voided with a reason (3 to 500 characters, stored in `notes`), `status` becomes `void`, the number is never reused and the PDF stays in R2. A void row is outside the partial unique index of invariant 2, so a corrected invoice can then be issued for the same submission, which stays in `Invoice Issued` until the new invoice is issued and then continues as normal. Only `admin`, human only; a `paid` or `waived` payment cannot be voided. Waive: needs a reason (3 to 500 characters, stored in `notes`). ASSUMED use for Five Features credits: the credit balance is not tracked in v1; a later property from an agent who bought Five Features is waived with reason "Five Features credit" (S3, exposure terms: declined properties do not use a credit, and a declined property never reaches an invoice).

- The Edge Function and the Worker both reach R2 through the S3 API in `r2-s3.ts` with the same secrets; there is no Worker binding, so `wrangler.toml` does not change. Invoices live in their own private bucket (`R2_INVOICES_BUCKET`), never in the public media bucket. R2 is off (E8): no bucket exists, so the upload, the download route and the email attachment are BLOCKED until the operator turns it on; B3's and B8's R2 steps share the same switch. While it is off, `invoice_pdf` jobs and invoice emails go `dead` after five attempts and are retried from screen 16 once R2 is on.
- The invoice email is only as fast as the runner tick (up to a minute). The toast shows `JobWatcher`; the admin can open the PDF as soon as the `invoice_pdf` job finishes.
- Disabling the `send_email(invoice)` step in B8b screen 17 stops the invoice email but not the PDF (own job). Intended.
- `Five Features` credit tracking, refunds and partial payments are not modelled. A refund is a manual event recorded in `notes` until a decision.

## Out of scope
A real Stripe adapter and webhook, refunds and the `refunded` transition, credit notes, taxes, multi-currency, recurring or instalment billing, Five Features credit balance, brand fonts in the PDF, the Invoicing section UI of screen 24 (B7 step 15 renders it from this plan's `invoiceSettingsSchema` through `InvoicingSection.tsx`), reports (screen 22), automatic reminders for overdue invoices, computing or collecting any tax (the tax line is a placeholder), a late-fee calculation, print styles for dossiers and the reduced-motion rule (B7), a Print button (the browser's print command and the PDF are enough).
