## Goal and observed exit
Money is a box with two doors (tech-stack 2, S32): acceptance never depends on payment, activation does. An accepted submission gets one numbered invoice from `/admin`, a PDF made in the job runner with `pdf-lib`, emailed to the agent through the seeded recipe; an admin marks it paid (or waives it) and activates the agent, which moves the submission to `Scheduled` and creates the property draft. Stripe can later write the same row through the same functions (S14 is superseded by S32 for launch).
Observed exit (completion map B6): "an admin issues an invoice and activates a test agent". Proven as `bunx playwright test tests/e2e/admin-invoice.spec.ts` on the preview stack: an accepted test request gets invoice `MOP-<year>-0001`, the PDF exists in R2, the email with the PDF arrives at a test inbox, mark paid then activate leaves the submission `Scheduled` with a property draft, and issuing on an unaccepted request is refused at the UI, the server function and the database.

## Contract (inputs, outputs, invariants, events emitted, permissions)
Inputs
- Server functions of architecture 4.2 `payments` (issue-invoice, mark-paid, waive, list, get, pdf) and `submissions.activate`, each mapped 1:1 to a route under `/api/admin/`.
- `settings.invoice` (ASSUMED key, jsonb): `{ prefix: "MOP", due_days: 14, terms: "<text>", late_terms: "<text>", tax_line: "<text>", payment_methods: [{ id, label, instructions }] }` (`late_terms` and `tax_line` are the GP-06 legal lines). Screen 24 has "invoice numbering prefix" (admin-screens 24); terms, due days and methods are the A4 owner inputs (bank, wire, card by phone). Seed: prefix `MOP`, `due_days` 14 (matches the 14-day overdue highlight of screen 5), `terms` "Payable within 14 days of the invoice date." (ASSUMED wording), three methods `bank_transfer`, `wire`, `card_by_phone` with EMPTY instructions, `tax_line` "No sales tax is charged on this invoice." (ASSUMED: the product is a service and no US sales tax is applied unless the accountant or lawyer says otherwise; a placeholder line, nothing is computed), `late_terms` "Payment is due by the date shown. Overdue amounts may be subject to a late charge." (ASSUMED wording with no rate invented; lawyer review is a HARDEN gate).
- Price source is `src/data/exposure.ts` only (S3, admin-screens key flows): `priceFor(product)` parses the `price` string of the matching offering (`$295` The Feature, `$695` The Reach, `$1,495` The Campaign, `$1,250` Five Features). The amount is never typed by a person (ASSUMED; a different price means editing the file). `Not sure yet` has no price and must be resolved to a real product at issue.

Outputs
- `payments` (architecture 3.4): `id`, `submission_id`, `property_id`, `product exposure_package`, `amount numeric(12,2)`, `currency char(3)` USD check, `method` (`invoice_manual|stripe`), `invoice_number text unique`, `invoice_r2_key`, `preferred_method text`, `status` (`due|paid|waived|refunded`), `issued_by`, `issued_at`, `paid_marked_by`, `paid_at`, `stripe_payment_intent`, `notes`. ASSUMED additions, to be written into architecture 3.4 in the same commit (G-004): `due_at timestamptz` (issue time plus `due_days`, stored so a later settings change does not move old invoices), `paid_method text`, `paid_reference text` (screen 6 "Mark paid (date, method, reference)"), `waived_by uuid`, `invoice_snapshot jsonb` (frozen at issue so a re-render years later is identical; GP-06 fields: `entity`, `address`, `contact`, `invoice_number`, `issue_date`, `due_date`, `description` (product name and its `line`), `amount`, `currency`, `tax_line`, `instructions`, `terms`, `late_terms`, plus the bill-to block), `created_at`, `updated_at`. B2 (`20261001090600_commercial.sql`) already creates `payments`, `campaigns` (with `payment_id`) and `campaign_reports`; this slice ALTERS `payments` to add the columns marked ASSUMED below.
- `invoice_number` = `<prefix>-<YYYY>-<NNNN>`, gapless per UTC year (ASSUMED UTC). Architecture says "from a sequence"; a Postgres sequence leaves gaps on rollback and does not reset yearly, so the function uses `invoice_counters(year, last)` with `insert ... on conflict (year) do update set last = invoice_counters.last + 1 returning last`, inside the issuing transaction. B2 created `next_invoice_number()` from a sequence and assumed the number does not reset each year; this slice REPLACES the function (`create or replace`) with the counter-table version. Numbering per year is ASSUMED from the `MOP-YYYY-NNNN` format in architecture 3.4.
- PDF: US Letter, one page, `pdf-lib` standard fonts (Times Roman headings, Helvetica text; ASSUMED, brand fonts are a follow-up), colours from `theme.gen.ts` (B5). Stored in R2 at `invoices/<year>/<invoice_number>.pdf`, private. Content: wordmark and "A product of Omnikom."; invoice number, issue date, due date; From (entity, address, contact email and phone from `settings.site`); Bill to (agent name, brokerage, email); Property (address, city, state, zip); the description line (product name and its `line` from `exposure.ts`) with its amount; the tax line from the snapshot (`tax_line`, a placeholder, no tax computed); total with the currency code `USD`; payment terms and the late terms; preferred payment method and the instructions of every listed method; closing line "Editorial acceptance precedes this invoice." (ASSUMED wording).
- Events (all existing catalog types, no new one): `invoice.issued` payload `{ submission_id, payment_id, invoice_number, tier }`; `payment.marked` payload `{ submission_id, payment_id, status: "paid"|"waived", amount }` (waive reuses this type; there is no `payment.waived` in the catalog); `submission.activated` payload `{ submission_id, property_id, tier, market }`. Recipes then send the invoice email (`send_email` template `invoice`, `attach: invoice_pdf`) and notify admins.
- Submission states moved: `Accepted` to `Invoice Issued` (issue), `Invoice Issued` to `Scheduled` (activate). The state name is `Invoice Issued` (architecture 3.3); `src/domain/contracts.ts` `submissionStates` still says `Awaiting Payment` and is corrected by B7 step 4.

Invariants
1. Acceptance before invoice, in three layers: the button is disabled in the UI; the server function refuses with 409 `not_accepted`; B2's trigger `enforce_editorial_gate` (migration `20261001090700_editorial_gate.sql`) raises for `Invoice Issued` and later states when `accepted_at` is null, and for `Scheduled` and later when no `payments` row for the submission has status `paid` or `waived` (architecture 3.4). B6 does not redefine the trigger; `tests/db/payments.db.test.ts` re-asserts it through the real functions so a regression in B2 shows here. B2's transition graph is mirrored in `src/domain/workflow.ts` (`canTransition`), which the service uses.
2. One live payment per submission: partial unique index on `payments(submission_id) where status in ('due','paid','waived')`.
3. Every state change is one SQL function (`issue_invoice`, `mark_payment_paid`, `waive_payment`, `activate_submission`) that updates the row, writes `audit_log` through `write_audit` and calls `emit_event` in the same transaction (supabase-js has no multi-statement transactions; the TS server function does actor, authz, Zod and then one RPC).
4. The invoice number is assigned in the issuing transaction and never changes. A rolled-back issue consumes no number.
5. The invoice is frozen: the PDF is rendered from `payments.invoice_snapshot`, not from live settings.
6. Nothing external in a request: the server function inserts rows and an `invoice_pdf` job; the runner renders, uploads and emails.
7. Issuing requires readiness: `settings.site` has `legal.entity`, `legal.address` and `contact.email`, and `settings.invoice.payment_methods` has at least one method with non-empty instructions. Also `settings.invoice.terms`, `late_terms` and `tax_line` must be non-empty (seeded, so this fails only if someone blanks them). Otherwise 409 `invoice_not_ready` with the missing list, and the screen shows it (no invented address, S33).
8. Activation requires status `paid` or `waived`, submission state `Invoice Issued`; it sets `activated_by` and `activated_at`, creates the property draft with `create_property_from_submission` when `property_id` is null, and is idempotent (a second call returns the same result, no second property).
9. Stripe is a stub interface only. Registry `adapters/index.ts` contains only `invoice_manual`; `stripe.ts` implements the interface with methods that throw `NotImplementedError`. When Stripe arrives it adds a webhook route that verifies the signature and calls `mark_payment_paid` with a null actor and `paid_method = 'stripe'`; the state machine and screens do not change.
10. Legal fields (GP-06, architecture 10): an issued invoice shows entity, address, invoice number, issue date, due date, description, amount, currency, a tax line, payment instructions and late terms, in the PDF and in the admin preview. The list is one constant, `requiredInvoiceFields`, in `src/domain/payments.ts`; `invoice-layout.test.ts` fails when `layoutInvoice` omits any of them for a complete snapshot, and `buildInvoiceSnapshot` throws when a required value is missing. Tax is a placeholder line only: nothing is computed, and nothing in the code names a rate.
11. Print (GQ-06): the admin invoice pages print cleanly on US Letter. `src/styles/admin/invoices.css` has an `@media print` block that hides every admin shell element marked `data-print="hide"` (ASSUMED attribute that B7's shell puts on navigation, toolbars and buttons), shows the preview at full width in `--foreground` on `--background` with no shadows or fixed positioning, sets `@page { size: letter; margin: 0.75in; }` (literal lengths, because `@page` cannot read custom properties; ASSUMED acceptable under G-007 since no colour is involved), and keeps the invoice block on one page (`break-inside: avoid`). The PDF stays the official copy; printing the preview is a convenience and is labelled Preview.

Waive: needs a reason (3 to 500 characters, stored in `notes`). ASSUMED use for Five Features credits: the credit balance is not tracked in v1; a later property from an agent who bought Five Features is waived with reason "Five Features credit" (S3, exposure terms: declined properties do not use a credit, and a declined property never reaches an invoice).

## Risks and gotchas (link GOTCHAS ids)
- G-004: `payments` columns, `src/domain/payments.ts` and the API JSON are one shape; the added columns are spec changes to architecture 3.4.
- G-005: the pricing reader in `src/server` is the second allowed importer of `src/data/exposure.ts`; routes and admin components still never import it and get the amount from the API.
- G-006 and G-011: R2 S3 credentials are server secrets in Supabase function secrets and GitHub only, never `VITE_*`; no paid service added (`pdf-lib` and `aws4fetch` are libraries).
- P-009: a PDF render is well under a second of Edge Function time; the Worker never renders (10 ms CPU per request on the free plan), which is why the `pdf` route only streams.
- No void action exists in the spec. An invoice issued in error cannot be reissued because of invariant 2. DECISION NEEDED: add `void` to `payment_status` and an admin-only action (recommended), or accept a runbook SQL correction. Until decided, only `waive` with reason exists.
- Numbering and tax: sequential numbers are provided; sales tax on media services and invoice content rules depend on the entity and state and belong to the CEO's accountant. Taxes are not computed. UNPROVEN legally.
- GP-06: the default `tax_line` and `late_terms` are ASSUMED wording chosen to claim nothing (no tax rate, no late fee rate). They are configuration, editable through `set-invoice.ts` and screen 24, and lawyer review of the final text is a HARDEN gate. An invoice already issued keeps its frozen text, so a later edit never changes an old invoice.
- GQ-06: `@page` margins are literal lengths because custom properties do not resolve there, the one place a literal dimension appears in admin CSS; no colour is involved, so the token rule holds for colour. The printed preview is HTML rendered by the browser and can differ from the PDF; the PDF is the record. Browsers add their own header and footer text to printed pages unless the user turns it off; nothing here can prevent that.
- GQ-06 and B7: `data-print="hide"` is a contract with B7's admin shell (ASSUMED attribute name). If B7 names it differently, `invoices.css` and `print-css.test.ts` change with it; until B7 step 3 lands the e2e print check cannot hide the real navigation and stays UNPROVEN.
- pdf-lib in Deno through `npm:` and standard fonts are UNPROVEN until step 4 runs on `mop-dev`. Standard fonts have no glyphs beyond WinAnsi: an agent name with characters outside it would throw; `sanitizeWinAnsi` replaces them with the nearest ASCII and logs (ASSUMED behaviour, tested in step 4).
- Edge Function to R2 needs S3 credentials; the Worker uses the R2 binding for the download route. Two access paths, one bucket.
- The invoice email is only as fast as the runner tick (up to a minute). The toast shows `JobWatcher`; the admin can open the PDF as soon as the `invoice_pdf` job finishes.
- Disabling the `send_email(invoice)` step in B8b screen 17 stops the invoice email but not the PDF (own job). Intended.
- `Five Features` credit tracking, refunds and partial payments are not modelled. A refund is a manual event recorded in `notes` until a decision.
