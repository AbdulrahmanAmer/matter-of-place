# Launch film v1 — storyboard

`matter-of-place-launch.mp4` · 72.6 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · sound design only (S36).
Built on `launch/engine` (one GSAP master timeline, seeked per frame). Times are absolute seconds on that timeline;
`scene.js` and `cues.json` use the same numbers. Moves are MOTION-BIBLE §2 IDs. Beat seconds stay inside ±30 % of §4.

Camera language for every photograph: three planes (back, subject under a soft radial mask, front under an edge mask)
that coincide at about a third of the move and diverge from there, so the push reads as parallax, not a zoom.

Rhythm: long holds (2.1 terrace 6 s, 7.1 threshold 6 s, 11.1 crane 6 s) against two rapid triplets (6.1 and 9.1,
0.4 s × 3). Silence twice (19.2–21.0, 60.0–64.4). Hard cuts land on a sound: the gust (7.6), the whoosh (11.2, 13.65,
16.25), the drop to silence (19.2, 60.0), the step (33.8, 40.4, 41.2), paper (37.0), fabric (40.8, 41.6).

Product: frames are the live site from a detached worktree of `main` on :8090, driven by `product-shots.mjs`, with the
site's own transitions off and every motion scripted per frame. Labels meant for the web preview ("ILLUSTRATIVE"),
prices and the floating concierge button are hidden by injected CSS/JS during capture only.

Photographs not used on purpose: `bayshore-residences`, `ridgeline-collection` (read as developments), desert images
(ambiguous market).
