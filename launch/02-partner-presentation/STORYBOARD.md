# Partner presentation — storyboard

`matter-of-place-for-partners.mp4` · 210.0 s (3:30) · 1920x1080 · 30 fps · 6300 frames · no voice, no sound.

Grammar: every slide is a full-bleed photograph drifting 2 percent, with a Bone or Warm Ivory panel (780 px wide,
inset 80 px) on alternating sides. Inside the panel: a Jost uppercase eyebrow, a Cormorant heading, then short lines.
Eyebrow and heading fade in at +1.2 s, body at +2.2 s, lists at +3.0 s (0.8 s each), then hold. Slides cross-fade
over 1.5 s. Holds are sized so every slide can be read twice at a calm pace. A folio sits at the panel foot
("Matter of Place · For partners" and "04 / 12"). All product names, prices, items, steps, qualities and
programmatic terms are copied from `src/data/exposure.ts`.

The slide windows are not typed by hand: `scene.html` lists each slide's hold (`data-hold`), and a small script
computes `data-in` / `data-out` before the engine runs (start 0.6 s, each next slide starts 1.5 s before the last ends).
