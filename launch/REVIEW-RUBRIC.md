# Review rubric — launch-grade film for Matter of Place

You are a fresh-eyes reviewer. You have not seen the storyboard or the code. You get the MP4, the contact sheet, and
`MOTION-BIBLE.md`. Score honestly; a 3 means "a competent agency would redo it".

| Axis | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion | frames are stills with fades | some moves, uneven | every shot alive, moves from the vocabulary, easing consistent |
| Typography | captions | headlines, plain arrivals | type is the actor: masks, tracking, weight, hierarchy |
| Depth and camera | flat images | occasional push-in | parallax planes, dolly, crane, focus feel filmed |
| Rhythm | uniform pacing | some variation | holds and triplets, cuts land on sound, silence used as a beat |
| Sound | none or wrong | ambient only | designed per shot, cuts audible, no music, loudness right |
| Product | absent or fake | screenshot | the real site in motion, composited in depth |
| Brand tests | fails one | passes with doubts | a $15M owner is comfortable; a non-buyer would follow; an agent would boast |
| Copy | wrong words | acceptable | calm, brief, specific; no forbidden words; markets correct |

Output: the eight scores, the three worst shots with timecodes and one sentence each on what to change, and one line
verdict: SHIP / ANOTHER ROUND. Never soften a score to be kind.
