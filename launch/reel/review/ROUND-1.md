Three worst shots
1. 2.9 to 7.0 s, interior with the headline: the depth planes tear at three edges, and the flat tan ceiling fills the top 200 px or so as a band. Reframe so the ceiling is out of frame, and extend or re-cut the foreground and ceiling planes so no edge shows at the full extent of the push.
2. 14.6 to 18.0 s, end card: the price is stacked on the lockup, the light sweep reads as a vertical haze over a mid-tone photograph (close to the banned glow), and the credit line is too small and on the band boundary. Give the price its own moment lower left with the facts or earlier in the shot, run the sweep diagonally on a darker frame or drop it, and raise and enlarge the credit.
3. 11.2 to 14.6 s, grid and colonnade: the tiles assemble in silence and the fabric sound arrives about 0.4 s late, then the grown colonnade shows a ghosted column edge. Move the fabric cue to the first tile at about 11.25 s, keep the silence to the black alone, and fix the left foreground plane of the colonnade.

Other points
- The opening 3 s is a soft picture with one small label and almost no visible camera travel, which is the weakest hook in the reel. A stronger push or an earlier headline would help.
