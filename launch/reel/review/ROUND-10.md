Three worst shots

1. 12.4 to 15.6 s, the concrete wall before the lock. For three seconds it is a plate with no type and almost no movement, only a slow defocus, so it reads as waiting for the end card. Cut it to about 1.5 s with a real dolly or crane and a timed 1 s rack, then bring the emblem assembly forward so the lock lands by about 14 s.
2. 8.75 to 10.1 s, the grid. Six tiles land and sit still on Obsidian for over a second, with the lower quarter of the frame (about 1460 to 1920 px) left empty. Bring the tiles in from offsets under a slow camera drift, centre the grid in the frame, start the grow within 0.4 s of the grid settling, and put the fabric cue on the grow.
3. 10.6 to 12.4 s, the colonnade after the grow. Once at full frame it is a frontal, centred one-point perspective that barely moves, so it reads as a still. Split it into planes (columns, opening, sea) and dolly through the opening, or give it a lateral crane so the columns pass across each other.

The opening needs a different cadence: hold one of the first three shots for 4 to 5 s and cut the others shorter so the reel does not tick at about 2.7 s per card.
At 2.8 s (frame 84), thin Sandstone slivers of the split panels show at the very top and bottom edges as they leave the frame. Clip the panels to the frame.
