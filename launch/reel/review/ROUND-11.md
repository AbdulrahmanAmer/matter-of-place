**Three worst shots**

1. **8.75 to 10.7 s, grid.**
   - What is wrong: six tiles sit nearly still in the upper part of a black frame (about 275 px empty above, 450 px below) for about 1.5 s, the gutters are uneven by a few pixels, and the fabric cue fires 0.45 s before the grow begins.
   - What to change: fill the vertical frame (3 by 3 or 2 by 4), keep each tile drifting slightly, shorten the hold to under 0.8 s, and put the fabric cue on the first frame of the grow.
2. **12.4 to 18.0 s, closing shot and end card.**
   - What is wrong: 5.6 s on one soft photograph of a wall, blurred and greyed behind mid-grey type, with the door and impact landing after the emblem and wordmark have already settled.
   - What to change: lock the end card on Obsidian, or on a darker plane that is still moving, then put the door on the frame the emblem's planes close and the impact on the last frame of the wordmark's mask.
3. **2.2 to 5.4 s, living room with the headline.**
   - What is wrong: the three-line headline sits over the sofa as a still caption for about 2.4 s, under a dark wash across the whole frame, and the whoosh at 5.2 s is over before the cut at 5.4 s.
   - What to change: let the headline counter-move against the push, or leave line by line before the cut, replace the full-frame wash with a local scrim, and end the whoosh on the cut frame.
