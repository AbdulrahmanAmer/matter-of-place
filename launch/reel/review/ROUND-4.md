Three worst shots

1. **3.5 to 5.3 s, headline over the living room.** The Bone headline lies across the cream sofa where "A residence" and "shaped" lose contrast, and at 5.27 s line one is half dissolved while lines two and three are intact when the hard cut lands. Place the block by luminance (or deepen the scrim behind it only), and finish a hard-mask exit of all three lines 0.3 s before the cut, or cut with the headline fully on.
2. **13.2 to 14.5 s, the price.** "$8,950,000" is thin and small, arrives plainly, and the wall's vertical edge travels under its last digits as the camera moves (edge at x 310 in frame 00392, 275 in 00415). Set it wholly on the wall or wholly on the sea, bring it in behind a rule or a mask, and consider joining it to the facts row so it is not the reel's closing argument.
3. **3.1 to 3.6 s, the split out of the oak.** At frame 00100 the opening gap is a 20 px strip of foliage that reads as a glitch line, the trunk does not register across the two panels, and its matte shows a pale halo. Dilate and feather the foreground matte, and open the split on a steeper ease so the first visible gap is a clear band, or lead it with a Sandstone rule.
