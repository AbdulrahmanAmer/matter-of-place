You are recalibrating an EXISTING website project for:

MATTER OF PLACE

Website:
matterofplace.com

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign this project from scratch.
Do NOT abandon the current visual identity, typography direction, spacing system, editorial restraint, luxury feel, subtle futurism, or existing design language.

The current project already visually represents the intended Matter of Place direction.

Your job is to evolve, refine, simplify, and commercially recalibrate the existing build around the business model below.

The desired result should feel like a mature editorial real-estate media company, not a startup landing page, generic luxury real-estate portal, brokerage website, ad agency, lead-generation company, or SaaS product.

Matter of Place is a highly curated real-estate media and distribution platform focused on exceptional existing residential properties.

The company operates ONLY across three primary markets:

CALIFORNIA
NEW YORK
FLORIDA

Do not introduce additional markets.

Do not imply global expansion on the live website.

Do not create international pages.

Do not create development-marketing services.

Do not position Matter of Place as a listing marketplace.

Do not position Matter of Place as a lead-generation service.

Matter of Place sits at the intersection of:

EDITORIAL +
REAL ESTATE +
DESIGN +
ARCHITECTURE +
CULTURE +
MEDIA DISTRIBUTION

The company’s central idea is:

Exceptional properties deserve exceptional attention.

Matter of Place identifies properties worth looking at, presents them through a disciplined editorial lens, and distributes them through owned media, social media, email, and precision programmatic advertising.

Matter of Place should feel more like:
